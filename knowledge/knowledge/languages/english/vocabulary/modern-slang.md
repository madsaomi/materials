# Modern English Buzzwords, Tech & AI Slang (2024–2026)

This lexicon covers modern terms that dominate contemporary tech culture, remote work environments, and internet discourse among Gen-Z and digital natives.

---

## 1. Top Modern Slang & Tech Lexicon

| Term | Meaning / Definition | Context & Real-life Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **delulu** | Delusional (often playfully optimistic) | Being unrealistically hopeful about an outcome: *"Being delulu is the solulu (solution) until you make it happen!"* |
| **rizz** | Charisma, charm in flirting | The natural ability to attract a romantic interest effortlessly: *"He's got unspoken rizz."* |
| **brainrot** | Cognitive fatigue from hyper-stimulating feeds | Mind-numbing consumption of endless short-form content (TikTok, Reels, meme-cycles): *"I've been scrolling for 3 hours, pure brainrot."* |
| **vibe check** | Assessing the mood or atmosphere | Checking if a person or environment is welcoming and aligned: *"He failed the vibe check instantly."* |
| **touch grass** | Go outside, disconnect from the screen | A reminder to leave the digital bubble and reconnect with reality: *"You've been arguing on Twitter all day, go touch grass."* |
| **quiet quitting** | Doing only the required work, no unpaid overtime | Setting firm boundaries at work without taking on extra unpaid burdens: *"She isn't stressed anymore; she's quietly quitting."* |
| **prompt engineering** | Crafting precise instructions for LLMs | The skill of structuring prompts to extract optimal outputs from AI models: *"Good prompt engineering cuts coding time in half."* |
| **hallucination (AI)** | Plausible-sounding false information from AI | When an LLM confidently invents facts, citations, or data: *"Double-check that API reference; it looks like an AI hallucination."* |
| **agentic** | Autonomous, goal-directed AI behavior | Systems capable of planning, using tools, and executing multi-step workflows: *"The new developer agent is fully agentic."* |
| **glow-up** | Major positive transformation | A dramatic improvement in appearance, confidence, or career: *"His career had a massive glow-up after learning to code."* |
| **living rent-free** | Obsessively occupying one's thoughts | A song, comment, or memory that you cannot stop thinking about: *"That catchy song has been living rent-free in my head all week."* |
| **red flag / beige flag** | Warning sign vs. quirky neutral trait | Red flag = serious flaw. Beige flag = mildly weird, harmless quirk: *"His beige flag is that he eats cereal with hot water."* |
| **ate and left no crumbs** | Performed flawlessly, crushed it | High praise for an exceptional performance, outfit, or presentation: *"Her keynote presentation ate and left no crumbs."* |
| **lore** | Backstory, history of a person or group | Personal or internet community history: *"You won't understand this joke unless you know our company lore."* |

## 2. Workplace & Tech Dialogue

**Alex:** Hey, did you review the pull request for the new customer analytics feature?  
**Sarah:** Yeah, the core logic is great, but the automated summary generated by the model had a minor **hallucination** regarding the billing cycle.  
**Alex:** Good catch! I'll refine the system prompt with better **prompt engineering** so it stays grounded.  
**Sarah:** Awesome. Also, remember we have a team **vibe check** at 4 PM, but no overtime today — it's Friday, let's log off on time and go **touch grass**!  
**Alex:** Agreed, 100%!
