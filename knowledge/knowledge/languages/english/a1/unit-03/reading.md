# Английский · Урок 03 — чтение: Сравнение вещей: цена и мнение · дополнительная версия

## Перед чтением

Это короткая учебная история. Сначала найдите участников и основную ситуацию без перевода. Затем отвечайте по сведениям текста: если данных нет, так и укажите.

## Текст для первого чтения

There are two bags in the shop. The red bag is old and cheap. It costs ten pounds. The blue bag is new and costs twenty pounds. The blue bag is more expensive than the red bag. Tom likes the red bag because it is small. Anna likes the blue bag because it is big. They like different things. The text gives no information about the quality of the bags.

## Вопросы без подсказки

1. Which bag is more expensive?
2. Why does Tom like the red bag?
3. Does the text prove that the blue bag is better quality?

## Полезные выражения

| Выражение | Значение в тексте |
|---|---|
| cheap | дешёвый |
| costs | стоит |
| more expensive than | дороже, чем |
| because | потому что |
| quality | качество |

## Разбор и перенос

Сравнение должно иметь понятную точку отсчёта. Не превращайте мнение героя или цену в недоказанный общий вывод.

Закройте текст и перескажите его тремя короткими фразами. Потом измените имя, место или количество и проверьте, какие формы должны измениться.

## Текст для чтения: Comparing Things

Tom and Anna are talking about their cities in a fictional conversation. Tom lives in London, and Anna lives in Moscow. Tom says London feels smaller and very expensive to him. Anna describes Moscow as bigger and expensive too. These are their impressions, not measured rankings.

"London is more international than Moscow," says Tom. "But the weather is worse. London is rainier."
"That's true," says Anna. "Moscow is colder in winter, but we have hotter summers."

Tom thinks Russian food is more delicious than English food. Anna thinks English breakfast is more interesting than Russian breakfast. They both agree that their cities are the most beautiful in the world.

"Is London better than Moscow for tourists?" asks Tom.
"Maybe. But Moscow is more historic," says Anna.

### Вопросы к тексту:

1. Where does Tom live?
2. Which city is bigger?
3. Can we tell which city is more expensive from this text alone? Explain.
4. What is Tom's opinion about Russian food?
5. What does Anna think about English breakfast?
6. Which city is colder in winter?

### Слова из текста:

- compare — сравнивать
- international — международный
- weather — погода
- rainier — дождливее (сравнительная степень)
- colder — холоднее
- hotter — жарче
- delicious — вкусный
- tourist — турист
- historic — исторический

# Unit 03 — Reading: Shopping (A1-A2 Level)

## Текст 1: Поход за покупками (Going Shopping)

On Saturday, Kate and her mum go to the city centre to do the shopping. First, they go to the supermarket to buy some food. Kate's mum needs a lot of things: bread, milk, eggs, some cheese and a few tomatoes. They also buy a little sugar and a lot of fruit.

"How much are the apples?" Kate asks the cashier.
"They're two pounds for a kilo," the cashier says.
"Can I have two kilos, please?"

After the supermarket, they go to a clothes shop because Kate needs a new jacket. There is a big sale this week — everything is twenty percent off. Kate finds a nice blue jacket. She tries it on, and it fits her perfectly.

"How much is this jacket?" Kate asks the sales assistant.
"It was forty pounds, but now it's thirty-two pounds with the discount," the assistant says.
"That's a great bargain!" Kate says. "I'll take it. Can I pay by card?"
"Of course," the assistant says.

At the end of the day, they are tired but very happy. Kate has a new jacket, and her mum has a basket full of food. They didn't spend too much money, which is good.

### Вопросы к тексту (Answer the questions):

1. When do Kate and her mum go shopping?
2. Where do they buy food?
3. How many kilos of apples do they buy?
4. What does Kate need from the clothes shop?
5. Why is the jacket cheaper? (What is the discount?)
6. How much was the jacket before and after the discount?
7. How does Kate pay?
8. Are they happy at the end of the day?

## Текст 2: Онлайн-шопинг (Online Shopping)

Mark prefers to shop online because he doesn't like queues and crowds. Last week, he wanted to buy a new pair of running shoes. He visited several online shops and compared the prices carefully.

In the first shop, the same shoes cost eighty dollars. In the second shop, they were seventy dollars but the delivery was not free. In the third shop, the shoes were sixty-five dollars, the delivery was free, and there was a small discount code.

"How much are the shoes in total?" Mark asked himself. "Sixty-five dollars with free delivery is the best price."

He put the shoes in the shopping cart and filled in his address. He chose to pay by card because it was safe and fast. After a few minutes, he received an email with the receipt and a tracking number.

The shoes arrived four days later. Mark tried them on. They fitted really well, and the colour was exactly like in the picture. He rated the shop five stars and wrote: "Great quality, fast delivery, good price. I'm happy with my purchase!"

Online shopping saves time, but Mark says you must check the sizes carefully because you can't try things on before buying.

### Вопросы к тексту (Answer the questions):

1. Why does Mark prefer to shop online?
2. What did Mark want to buy?
3. Which shop had the best price? Why?
4. How did Mark pay?
5. When did the shoes arrive?
6. Did Mark like the shoes? What did he write in his review?
7. What is the problem with online shopping according to Mark?

## Текст 3: Возврат в магазине (Returning an Item in a Shop)

Alex bought a pair of grey trousers last Monday. He paid sixty dollars for them. But when he got home, he realised the size was too small, so he couldn't wear them.

On Tuesday, Alex went back to the shop with the trousers and the receipt. He spoke to the customer service desk.

"Hello, I'd like to return these trousers, please," Alex said. "They're too small for me."

The assistant looked at the trousers and the receipt. "That's no problem. Would you like a refund or an exchange?"
"What's the difference?" Alex asked.
"With a refund, we give your money back. With an exchange, you choose something else of the same price," the assistant explained.

Alex liked a brown sweater in the shop. It cost the same — sixty dollars. "Actually, could I exchange them for this sweater, please?" he asked.
"Of course. Do you need a new receipt for the sweater?" the assistant asked.
"Yes, please," Alex said.

Alex left the shop happy. He learned an important lesson: always check the size before you buy, and keep the receipt!

### Вопросы к тексту (Answer the questions):

1. What did Alex buy last Monday?
2. How much did he pay?
3. Why did he want to return them?
4. What two options did the assistant give him?
5. What is the difference between a refund and an exchange?
6. What did Alex choose?
7. What lesson did Alex learn?

## Уточнение к расширенному тексту Comparing Things

На вопрос о том, какой город дороже, недостаточно данных: в разговоре нет сопоставимой цены или общего измерения стоимости. Субъективные more beautiful / better не выдаются за проверенный рейтинг городов.
