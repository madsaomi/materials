# Unit 06 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Weather structures, and -ed vs -ing participial adjectives

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### Dummy subject 'It'
Weather statements require 'It' as subject: 'It is raining' (NOT Rain goes), 'It is windy' (NOT There is wind).

### -ed (feelings) vs -ing (cause of feelings)
'I am bored' = я скучаю. 'I am boring' = я зануда/скучный человек! 'The movie was exciting, so the children were excited.'

### It is sunny vs The sun is shining
Both are natural; avoid literal Russian translations like 'Sun shines strong'.

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ The rain goes
  ✅ It is raining — English uses the dummy subject 'It' and the verb 'rain'.

- ❌ I am very boring in this lesson
  ✅ I am very bored in this lesson — '-ing' describes the characteristic, '-ed' describes personal emotion.
