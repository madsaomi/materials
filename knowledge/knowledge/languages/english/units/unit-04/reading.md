## Текст 1: The Smith Family (Семья Смитов)

### Текст (Text)

The Smith family lives in a small house in Boston. There are five people in the family. The father, John, is 45 years old. He is a kind and hardworking man. He works as an engineer. The mother, Mary, is 42. She is a teacher at the local school. She is patient and friendly.

They have three children. Tom, the eldest, is 18. He is tall and slim, like his father. He is going to college next year. Anna, the middle child, is 15. She has her mother's brown hair and blue eyes. She is very smart and loves reading. The youngest is Jack. He is 10. He is funny and energetic. He loves playing football with his friends.

The grandparents live nearby. Grandpa Joe is 70 and still very active. Grandma Rose is 68. They visit the family every Sunday for dinner. The whole family gets together and enjoys each other's company.

The Smith family is happy. They support each other in good times and bad times. They are a close and loving family.

## Текст 2: A Big Family (Большая семья)

### Текст (Text)

I come from a very big family. My parents have six children — three boys and three girls. I am the third child. My elder brother Victor is 25, and my elder sister Natasha is 24. My younger brothers and sisters are still at school.

My grandparents live in the same village as us, in a house next door to my parents. My grandmother bakes bread every morning, and the whole house smells wonderful. My grandfather keeps bees and sells honey at the local market.

About twenty relatives usually come to our birthday parties. Aunts, uncles, and cousins arrive from different towns. We cook together, tell stories, and play games. My grandmother loves it when the whole family is together. She says that a home without children is like a house without sunshine.

Sometimes the house gets noisy and crowded, but I wouldn't change anything. In a big family, you always have someone to talk to, someone to help you, and someone to love. Being part of a big family is a great blessing.

### Вопросы (Questions)

1. How many children do the writer's parents have?
   *(Сколько детей у родителей автора?)*

2. Which child is the writer?
   *(Каким по счёту ребёнком является автор?)*

3. Where do the grandparents live?
   *(Где живут бабушка и дедушка?)*

4. What does the grandmother bake every morning?
   *(Что бабушка печёт каждое утро?)*

5. What does the grandfather keep?
   *(Что держит дедушка?)*

6. How many relatives usually come to the birthday parties?
   *(Сколько родственников обычно приходит на дни рождения?)*

7. What does the grandmother say about a home without children?
   *(Что говорит бабушка о доме без детей?)*

8. Why does the writer like having a big family?
   *(Почему автору нравится иметь большую семью?)*

2. The writer is the third child.
3. They live in the same village, next door to the parents' house.
4. She bakes bread.
5. He keeps bees.
6. About twenty relatives come.
7. She says a home without children is like a house without sunshine.
8. Because you always have someone to talk to, help you, and love.

## Текст 3: Family Traditions (Семейные традиции)

### Текст (Text)

Every family has its own traditions, and ours are very important to us. The most important tradition in our family is Sunday dinner. Every Sunday, my mother cooks a big meal, and all of us — my father, my brother, my sister, and I — sit together at one table. We switch off our phones and just talk about our week.

Another tradition is celebrating birthdays. We don't buy expensive presents. Instead, we make gifts by hand or cook the person's favourite dish. My mother always bakes a cake for the birthday person.

At New Year, our whole extended family gathers. My uncles and aunts, and all the cousins, come to our house. My grandfather tells funny stories, and we sing songs together at midnight. We believe that spending time together makes our family stronger.

These traditions may seem simple, but they connect us. They teach children to respect their elders and to care about each other. I hope that one day my own children will continue these traditions too.

### Вопросы (Questions)

1. What is the most important tradition in the writer's family?
   *(Какая самая важная традиция в семье автора?)*

2. What do they do at Sunday dinner?
   *(Что они делают на воскресном ужине?)*

3. What presents do they give on birthdays?
   *(Какие подарки они дарят на дни рождения?)*

4. What does the mother bake for the birthday person?
   *(Что мама печёт для именинника?)*

5. Where does the extended family gather at New Year?
   *(Где собирается семья в Новый год?)*

6. What do these traditions teach children?
   *(Чему эти традиции учат детей?)*

7. What does the writer hope for the future?
   *(На что надеется автор в будущем?)*

### Ответы (Answers)

1. The most important tradition is Sunday dinner.
2. They switch off their phones and talk about their week.
3. They make gifts by hand or cook the person's favourite dish.
4. She bakes a cake.
5. They gather at the writer's (our) house.
6. They teach children to respect elders and care about each other.
7. The writer hopes his/her own children will continue these traditions.
