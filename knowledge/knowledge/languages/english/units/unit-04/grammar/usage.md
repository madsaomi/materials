# Unit 04 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Possessives ('s vs of), Have got, and Verbs of liking

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### Saxon Genitive ('s) for people/animals vs 'of' for inanimate things
'John's car', 'The cat's tail', BUT 'The roof of the house' (NOT the house's roof).

### Have got (British/conversational) vs Have (American/formal)
'Have you got a pen?' = 'Do you have a pen?' In the past, only 'had' is used ('I had a dog', never 'I had got').

### Gerund after verbs of liking
After like, love, enjoy, hate, prefer, use the -ing form for general preference: 'I enjoy swimming' (NOT I enjoy to swim).

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ The table's leg
  ✅ The leg of the table — Inanimate objects typically use 'of'.

- ❌ I enjoy to read books
  ✅ I enjoy reading books — 'Enjoy' is strictly followed by a gerund (-ing).
