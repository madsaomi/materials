# Unit 05 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Countable vs Uncountable nouns, and Food/Article nuances

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### Tricky uncountables for Russian speakers
Advice, information, news, furniture, luggage, bread, progress, money are strictly UNCOUNTABLE: 'He gave me some good advice' (NOT an advice, NOT advices). 'The news is good' (singular verb!).

### Many / Few (countable) vs Much / Little (uncountable)
Many friends, few cars. Much time, little water. 'A few' = several (positive); 'few' = almost none (negative).

### Articles with meals
No article for regular meals: 'Have breakfast', 'at lunch'. But use an article if there's an adjective: 'We had a delicious breakfast'.

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ He gave me an advice
  ✅ He gave me some advice / a piece of advice — 'Advice' is uncountable in English.

- ❌ The news are interesting
  ✅ The news is interesting — 'News' always takes a singular verb.
