## Текст для чтения 1: A Healthy Diet

Eating healthy is important for our bodies. A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, grains, proteins, and dairy products. We need to eat five portions of fruits and vegetables every day.

Breakfast is the most important meal of the day. A good breakfast includes whole grain cereal, milk, fruit, and maybe some eggs. It gives us energy for the morning. Lunch should be a balanced meal with protein, vegetables, and carbohydrates. Dinner should be light. We should avoid eating too much before bed.

Many people enjoy cooking. Cooking at home is healthier than eating out. We can control the ingredients and the amount of salt, sugar, and oil. Drinking enough water is also very important. We should drink about eight glasses of water a day.

Some people are vegetarians. They don't eat meat. Some people are vegans. They don't eat any animal products. Other people have food allergies. For example, some people are allergic to nuts, dairy, or gluten. It's important to read food labels and ask about ingredients in restaurants.

Eating healthy doesn't mean you can't enjoy your food. You can still have desserts and snacks in moderation. The key is balance and variety.

### Вопросы к тексту 1:

1. Why is eating healthy important?
2. How many portions of fruits and vegetables should we eat?
3. What should a good breakfast include?
4. Why is cooking at home better than eating out?
5. How many glasses of water should we drink a day?
6. What is the difference between vegetarians and vegans?
7. What is the key to a healthy diet?

## Текст для чтения 2: Eating Out Around the World

Eating out is a part of modern life. When people eat in a restaurant, they expect good service, tasty food, and fair prices. But every country has its own customs.

In the United States, a waiter brings the bill after the meal. Customers usually leave a tip of about 15–20 per cent. In Japan, people sometimes say «Itadakimasu» before eating. It means «I receive this food». In many Asian countries, people eat with chopsticks, and it is not polite to stick chopsticks upright in a bowl of rice.

In Italy, you do not order a cappuccino after dinner — Italians drink coffee like espresso after a meal. In Spain, dinner is usually late, often after 9 p.m., and lunch is the biggest meal of the day. In Russia, guests bring something sweet to a party, and the host says «Приятного аппетита». In English you can say «Enjoy your meal».

When you travel, it is useful to know these little rules. They help you feel comfortable and show respect for the local culture.

4. What do Italians prefer to drink after dinner?

6. What do Russian guests bring to a party?
7. How can you say «Приятного аппетита» in English?
8. Why is it useful to know local food rules when you travel?

## Текст для чтения 3: Cuisines of the World

Every country has a special cuisine — food that tells a story about its people and its climate. Italian cuisine is famous for pasta, pizza, and risotto. Italians use fresh tomatoes, olive oil, and basil. A traditional Italian meal starts with antipasti (appetizers), then comes the first course, and then the main course.

French cuisine is known for its sauces, cheese, and pastries. The French enjoy long meals and talk about food with pleasure. Japanese cuisine is healthy and fresh. Sushi and sashimi are made from raw fish. Japanese people value the beauty and simplicity of food.

Mexican food is spicy and colourful — tacos, burritos, and guacamole, and people often eat it with their hands. Indian cuisine is famous for curry; it is rich with spices and herbs. Russian cuisine includes soups like borscht, buckwheat, and dumplings. It is filling because of the cold climate. In winter, hot soup is perfect.

Food brings people together. When you try a new cuisine, you learn something new about a country. Travelling is not only about places — it is also about taste.
