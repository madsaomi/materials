## Упражнение 1: Countable or Uncountable — определите

1. apple — countable
2. water — uncountable
3. bread — uncountable
4. egg — countable
5. rice — uncountable
6. sandwich — countable
7. milk — uncountable
8. pizza — countable (a whole pizza, but: a slice of pizza)
9. sugar — uncountable
10. tea — uncountable (but: a cup of tea)

## Упражнение 2: some / any — заполните

1. I have ___ money. → some
2. There isn't ___ milk in the fridge. → any
3. Would you like ___ coffee? → some
4. Do you have ___ brothers or sisters? → any
5. She doesn't eat ___ meat. → any

## Упражнение 3: much / many / a lot of — заполните

1. I don't have ___ friends. → many
2. There isn't ___ water. → much
3. He has ___ money. → a lot of
4. How ___ apples do you want? → many
5. How ___ sugar do you need? → much

## Упражнение 4: a few / few / a little / little — заполните

1. I have ___ friends. (несколько) → a few
2. I have ___ friends. (почти нет) → few
3. There is ___ milk. (немного) → a little
4. There is ___ milk. (почти нет) → little
5. We need ___ more time. → a little

## Упражнение 5: Перевод на английский

1. У меня много друзей. — I have a lot of friends.
2. Сколько воды тебе нужно? — How much water do you need?
3. Я хочу чашку чая. — I want a cup of tea.
4. У нас нет хлеба. — We don't have any bread.
5. Я ем фрукты каждый день. — I eat fruit every day.

## Расширенная практика

## Упражнение 6: Фразы в ресторане — переведите на английский

1. Можно меню, пожалуйста? → Can I have the menu, please? / The menu, please.
2. Можно счёт, пожалуйста? → The bill, please. / Could I have the bill, please?
3. Что вы порекомендуете? → What do you recommend?
4. Я вегетарианец. → I'm a vegetarian.
5. Давай разделим счёт. → Let's split the bill.

## Упражнение 7: like или would like — заполните

1. I ___ coffee very much. → like
2. I ___ a cup of tea, please. → would like / 'd like
3. ___ you ___ to order a dessert? → Would / like
4. Do you ___ spicy food? → like
5. At the café she ___ a piece of cake. → 'd like

## Упражнение 8: Can I / Could I / Can you / Could you — выберите

1. ___ have the menu, please? → Can/Could I
2. ___ pass the salt, please? → Can/Could you
3. ___ have a glass of water? → Can/Could I
4. ___ open the window? It's hot here. → Can/Could you
5. ___ borrow your phone, please? → Can/Could I

## Упражнение 9: How much / How many — заполните

3. ___ bottles of water should I buy? → How many
4. ___ rice is left in the bag? → How much
5. ___ people are coming for dinner? → How many

## Упражнение 10: A / An / The / — (без артикля) с едой

2. We had ___ lovely dinner last night. → a
3. ___ dinner at the hotel was delicious. → The
4. She usually has ___ sandwich for lunch. → a
5. ___ lunch we had yesterday was terrible. → The

## Упражнение 11: Present Simple — поставьте глагол в правильную форму

1. She (eat) ___ rice every day. → eats
2. I (drink) ___ tea in the morning. → drink
3. He (not/like) ___ spicy food. → doesn't like
4. (you/order) ___ pizza often? → Do you order
5. They (cook) ___ dinner at seven every day. → cook

## Упражнение 12: Найдите и исправьте ошибку

1. I have many bread. → I have a lot of bread.
2. She like apples. → She likes apples.
3. How much eggs do you want? → How many eggs do you want?
4. Can I have a milk, please? → Can I have some milk, please?
5. I'd like to have some informations. → I'd like to have some information.

## Упражнение 16: Выражения и идиомы — сопоставьте

## Упражнение 17: Present Continuous или Present Simple — выберите

1. She (cooks / is cooking) dinner now. → is cooking
2. He (eats / is eating) out every Friday. → eats
3. Look! The waiter (brings / is bringing) our drinks. → is bringing
4. We usually (have / are having) breakfast at 8. → have
5. I (make / am making) a cake at the moment. → am making
6. They (don't eat / aren't eating) meat now — they are vegetarian. → aren't eating

## Упражнение 18: Исправьте распространённые ошибки

1. I bought a bread. → I bought a loaf of bread. / some bread.
2. Can I have an advice? → Can I have some advice?
3. How many money do you have? → How much money do you have?
4. She drink coffee every morning. → She drinks coffee every morning.
5. I would like the dessert after dinner. → I would like a dessert after dinner. (или the dessert = конкретный десерт из меню)

5. ___ — My mother does. → Who cooks in your family?
6. ___ — Salads and soup. → What do you usually have for lunch?
7. ___ — Twice a week. → How often do you order pizza?

## Упражнение 20: Ролевая игра — составить диалог
