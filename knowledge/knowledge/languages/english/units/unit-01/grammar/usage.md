# Unit 01 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** To be in Present Simple, Question Order, and Age/State traps

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### The 'Have vs Be' Trap for Russian speakers
In Russian: «Мне 20 лет», «Я голоден», «Я прав». In English, these are states with 'to be': 'I am 20 years old' (NOT I have 20 years), 'I am hungry' (NOT I have hunger), 'I am right' (NOT I have right).

### Question word order: ASI (Auxiliary - Subject - Infinitive) & QUASI
Question word + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb: 'Where do you live?' 'What does he do?' With 'to be', simply invert: 'Are you ready?'

### Short answers: Yes, I am / No, I'm not
Avoid answering with just 'Yes' or 'No'. Use proper tags: 'Are you from Russia? — Yes, I am.'

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ I have 25 years old
  ✅ I am 25 years old — Age is always expressed with the verb 'to be'.

- ❌ Where you work?
  ✅ Where do you work? — General questions in Present Simple require the auxiliary 'do/does'.
