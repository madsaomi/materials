# Unit 08 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Time prepositions (at, on, in) and Schedule expressions

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### At, On, In for Time
'At' for exact times (at 5:00, at midnight, at the weekend - UK). 'On' for days and dates (on Monday, on May 5th, on my birthday). 'In' for longer periods (in July, in 2026, in summer, in the morning).

### No prepositions before: last, next, every, this
❌ in next week -> ✅ next week. ❌ on every day -> ✅ every day. ❌ in this morning -> ✅ this morning.

### Present Simple for fixed schedules
'The train leaves at 7:00 tomorrow morning' — Use Present Simple, not Future, for timetables and official schedules.

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ On the next week I will go
  ✅ Next week I will go — Words like 'next', 'last', 'every' drop prepositions.

- ❌ In Monday
  ✅ On Monday — Days of the week always take 'on'.
