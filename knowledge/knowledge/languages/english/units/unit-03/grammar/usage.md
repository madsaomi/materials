# Unit 03 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Adjectives vs Adverbs, and Irregular Comparisons

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### Adverbs modifying verbs vs Adjectives with linking verbs
Verbs of sense (look, smell, sound, taste, feel) take ADJECTIVES, not adverbs: 'You look beautiful' (NOT beautifully), 'The food smells delicious' (NOT deliciously).

### Irregular adverbs: Good/Well, Fast/Fast, Hard/Hardly
'He is a hard worker' (he works hard). 'He hardly works' means 'he almost doesn't work at all' (едва ли работает)!

### Double comparatives are strictly forbidden
Never say 'more better' or 'more easier'. Use either '-er' or 'more', never both.

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ I feel myself badly
  ✅ I feel bad / unwell — In English 'feel myself' has a literal sexual connotation, and linking verbs take adjectives.

- ❌ It is more cheaper
  ✅ It is cheaper (or much cheaper) — Do not double the comparative marker.
