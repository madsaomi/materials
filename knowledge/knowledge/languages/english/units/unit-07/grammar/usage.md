# Unit 07 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Prepositions of Place (in, on, at) and Movement (into, through, across)

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### At vs In vs On (The Pyramid rule)
'At' for specific points/events (at the bus stop, at a party, at work). 'On' for surfaces and streets (on the table, on Oxford Street, on a bus). 'In' for enclosed spaces, cities, and countries (in the room, in London, in a car).

### Public transport (on) vs Private car (in)
'On the bus / train / plane' (you can stand up/walk). 'In the car / taxi' (you must sit inside).

### Arrive at vs Arrive in
Arrive in a city/country ('arrive in New York'). Arrive at a specific building/station ('arrive at the airport'). NEVER 'arrive to'!

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ I arrived to the airport
  ✅ I arrived at the airport — 'Arrive' never takes the preposition 'to'.

- ❌ I am in the bus
  ✅ I am on the bus — Public transport takes 'on'.
