# Unit 15 — Итоговое чтение: 3 текста уровня B1–B2 с вопросами

Формат работы: читай текст 2 раза (первый — бегло, второй — внимательно), затем отвечай на вопросы.
Время на каждый текст: 15–20 минут. Ответы в конце каждого текста.

## Текст 1. «A Journey Across Canada» (путешествия, блоки 11 и 14)

Last summer my friend Mark and I flew to Canada for a three-week trip across the country. We began in Toronto, a busy city famous for its multicultural population and the CN Tower. From there, we took a train through the province of Ontario, watching endless forests and blue lakes pass by the window.

Our next stop was the Rocky Mountains in the province of Alberta. We spent four days hiking on mountain trails and sleeping in a small wooden cabin near a river. The air was fresh and the sun was warm during the day, but every night the temperature dropped below freezing. "You need a warm sleeping bag," the local guide warned us, and he was absolutely right.

After that, we travelled west to Vancouver, on the Pacific coast. Vancouver is surrounded by both mountains and the sea, so locals can ski in the morning and swim in the afternoon. The city is also known for its green technology and eco-friendly transport. In fact, the bus we took pulled up with zero noise because the whole fleet is electric.

Our final destination was a tiny fishing village on Vancouver Island. There we met a retired fisherman who had lived there for forty years. "I came for one season," he laughed, "and never left." He cooked us the freshest salmon I have ever eaten. The whole journey lasted three weeks and cost less than I had expected. I would definitely go back, only next time I would pack lighter luggage.

### Вопросы к тексту 1

1. Where did the narrator and Mark begin their trip?
2. How did they travel from Toronto to the Rocky Mountains?
3. Why was the local guide's warning important?
4. What is special about transport in Vancouver?
5. Who did they meet on Vancouver Island?
6. What did the fisherman cook for them?
7. How long did the whole journey last?
8. What would the narrator do differently next time?

### Ответы к тексту 1

1. They began in Toronto, a busy city famous for its multicultural population and the CN Tower.
2. They took a train through the province of Ontario.
3. Because the temperature dropped below freezing at night in the mountains, so they needed a warm sleeping bag.
4. It is green and eco-friendly; the whole bus fleet is electric and makes no noise.
5. A retired fisherman who had lived there for forty years.
6. He cooked them the freshest salmon the narrator had ever eaten.
7. The whole journey lasted three weeks.
8. He would pack lighter luggage.

## Текст 2. «How Social Media Changed Our Lives» (технологии, блок 13)

Social media has completely changed the way we communicate. Twenty years ago, if you wanted to tell your friends about your holiday, you printed your photos and showed them in person. Today, people upload hundreds of pictures at once, and a single funny video can go viral around the world in a matter of hours.

Hundreds of millions of people use social networks every day, and the average user spends about two and a half hours online on social platforms. On the one hand, this is very convenient: you can stay in touch with relatives in other countries, follow the news in real time, and find communities of people who share your hobbies. On the other hand, endless scrolling can cause serious problems. Researchers warn that too much time in front of a screen weakens concentration, and comparing your life with carefully edited photos of others can increase stress and anxiety.

Another important issue is privacy. When you create an account, you often give away personal data without reading the terms and conditions. Experts recommend using strong passwords, checking privacy settings regularly, and thinking carefully before posting anything that could damage your reputation in the future. After all, the internet never forgets.

The future of social media depends on us. If we learn to use it wisely, it can remain a powerful tool for communication and creativity. If we ignore the risks, the same networks may become a source of constant distraction. The choice, as always, is ours.

### Вопросы к тексту 2

1. How did people share holiday photos twenty years ago?
2. How much time does the average user spend on social platforms daily?
3. What are two positive effects of social media mentioned in the text?
4. What two problems can too much screen time cause?
5. Why is strong password important according to the text?
6. What did the author mean by saying «the internet never forgets»?
7. What does the future of social media depend on?

### Ответы к тексту 2

1. They printed their photos and showed them in person.
2. About two and a half hours a day.
3. You can stay in touch with relatives, follow the news in real time, and find communities of people who share your hobbies.
4. It weakens concentration and can increase stress and anxiety.
5. Because you often give away personal data when creating an account, so you need to protect it.
6. Information posted online stays there forever and can damage your reputation later.
7. The future depends on us — on whether we use social media wisely or ignore the risks.

## Текст 3. «The United Kingdom: One Country, Four Nations» (история и география, блок 14)

The United Kingdom is a union of four nations: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Many people confuse the terms "the UK", "Great Britain" and "England", but they are not the same thing. Great Britain is the island that contains England, Scotland and Wales, while the UK also includes Northern Ireland.

Each nation has its own identity, traditions and even languages. In Wales, for example, the Welsh language, which belongs to the Celtic family, is still spoken by about a million people. Scotland has its own legal system, its own education system and its own banknotes, which are fully accepted all over the UK. Northern Ireland's capital, Belfast, became a major shipbuilding centre in the 19th century — the famous liner Titanic was built there in 1912.

London, the capital of the whole kingdom, has been an important city for more than two thousand years. It was founded by the Romans as a trading settlement called Londinium. Today London is one of the world's financial capitals, although the seat of government and the official residence of the monarch are actually in Westminster.

The climate of the UK is often described as "mild and changeable": it rarely gets extremely hot in summer or very cold in winter, but rain can arrive at any moment. This explains why talking about the weather is such an important part of British conversation — it is always a safe topic.

### Вопросы к тексту 3

1. What four nations form the United Kingdom?
2. What is the difference between Great Britain and the UK?
3. How many people still speak Welsh?
4. What famous ship was built in Belfast?
5. When and how was London founded?
6. What does "mild and changeable" describe in the text?
7. Why is talking about the weather a safe topic in Britain?

### Ответы к тексту 3

1. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
2. Great Britain is the island with England, Scotland and Wales; the UK also includes Northern Ireland.
3. About a million people.
4. The liner Titanic, built in 1912.
5. London was founded by the Romans more than two thousand years ago as a trading settlement called Londinium.
6. The climate of the UK — mild but changeable, with rain at any moment.
7. Because the weather changes so often that it is always a safe topic for conversation.
