# Unit 13 — Тонкости грамматики и типичные ошибки (English)

> 📌 **Тема юнита:** Modals of obligation: Must vs Have to vs Should, and Tech vocabulary

## 1. Прагматические нюансы и скрытые правила

### Must (internal conviction / strong personal rule) vs Have to (external rule / circumstance)
'I must call my mother' (my own desire/duty). 'I have to wear a uniform at work' (company rules require it).

### DRAMATIC contrast: Mustn't vs Don't have to
'You mustn't touch this' = Запрещено! (Categorical prohibition). 'You don't have to come' = Не обязательно, можешь не приходить (Lack of obligation). Mixing these up causes severe misunderstandings!

### Should / Ought to for recommendations and advice
'You should change your password regularly' (Best practice/friendly recommendation).

---

## 2. Ловушки перевода и типичные ошибки русскоязычных учащихся

- ❌ You mustn't pay, it's free
  ✅ You don't have to pay, it's free — 'Mustn't' means forbidden! 'Don't have to' means no necessity.

- ❌ In the past I must work late
  ✅ In the past I had to work late — 'Must' has no past tense form; 'had to' must be used.
