# Английский · Урок 09 — чтение: Спортивный клуб: опыт и отдельное событие · дополнительная версия

## Перед чтением

Это короткая учебная история. Сначала найдите участников и основную ситуацию без перевода. Затем отвечайте по сведениям текста: если данных нет, так и укажите.

## Текст для первого чтения

Nora has tried badminton, but she has never played tennis. She first played badminton last Saturday at a local club. Her friend Ivo showed her how to hold the racket. Nora lost their first game, but she enjoyed it. Ivo plays at the club on Tuesdays and Thursdays. He has played badminton for two years. Nora wants to visit again next week. The story does not tell us who is the best player in the club.

## Вопросы без подсказки

1. Has Nora ever played tennis, according to the text?
2. When did she first play badminton?
3. How many days a week does Ivo play at the club?
4. Does losing one game prove who is the best player in the club?

## Полезные выражения

| Выражение | Значение в тексте |

| has tried | имеет опыт, пробовала |
| has never played | до настоящего момента ни разу не играла |
| last Saturday | в прошлую субботу |
| racket | ракетка |
| lost | проиграла, форма lose |

## Проверка понимания

<details>
<summary>Открыть ответы и объяснения</summary>

1. No, she hasn’t. She has never played tennis. Это явное сообщение текста, а не отсутствие сведений.
2. Last Saturday. Дано завершённое прошлое время; вопрос использует did ... play, ответ относится к конкретному событию.
3. Two: Tuesdays and Thursdays. Это twice a week; слово often само по себе не задаёт точного числа.
4. No. The story gives no club ranking. Одно поражение Норы не доказывает место всех участников в рейтинге.

## Разбор и перенос

Present Perfect связывает опыт или продолжающийся период с настоящим; Past Simple располагает отдельный эпизод в завершённом прошлом. Поражение, привычка и жизненный опыт — разные сведения.

Закройте текст и перескажите его тремя короткими фразами. Потом измените имя, место или количество и проверьте, какие формы должны измениться.

## Текст 1: A Healthy Lifestyle (Здоровый образ жизни)

Anna is writing about her own daily routine. On Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays she walks for thirty minutes. She enjoys these walks, but she does not call ninety minutes a universal exercise target. Different activities and individual circumstances need separate consideration.

Anna likes fruit and vegetables and carries a bottle of water. She usually eats breakfast before work because this routine suits her. Her friend eats at different times. Anna does not use their meal times to predict anyone’s weight or health.

She usually goes to bed at eleven and gets up at seven. This gives her an eight-hour period between bedtime and getting up; the story does not measure how long she actually sleeps. When she wants a break, she takes a short walk or reads a book.

Anna describes habits that she enjoys. This is a personal account, not a fixed prescription of exercise, water or sleep for every reader.

### Вопросы к тексту 1

1. On which days does Anna walk, and for how long each time?
2. What food and drink does Anna mention?
3. Why does Anna usually eat breakfast before work?
4. Does the eight-hour period prove that Anna sleeps for exactly eight hours? Explain.
5. What does Anna do when she wants a break?
6. Is Anna’s routine presented as a prescription for every reader?

## Текст 2: An Injury During Training (Травма на тренировке)

Last Saturday, Mark went to the gym as usual. He was preparing for a local basketball tournament and practised very hard. He wanted to improve his jumping, so he was doing some exercises on the court late in the evening.

Suddenly, Mark landed badly after a jump and felt a sharp pain in his right ankle. He couldn't stand up. His friend Tom, who was playing nearby, ran to help him. "Are you all right?" Tom asked. "I think I've sprained my ankle," Mark replied. Tom immediately called an ambulance, and Mark was taken to the hospital.

At the hospital, a doctor examined Mark's ankle and ordered an X-ray. Fortunately, nothing was broken, but the ligaments were stretched. The doctor put a bandage on the ankle and gave Mark some painkillers. "You should rest for at least two weeks," the doctor said. "Don't play basketball until the swelling goes down."

Mark was disappointed because his team had an important match the following week. However, he understood that his health was more important than one game. He decided to follow the doctor's advice and started doing light exercises for his upper body to stay fit while his ankle healed.

### Вопросы к тексту 2

1. What was Mark preparing for?
2. What happened to Mark during the training?
3. Who helped Mark after the injury?
4. What did the doctor do at the hospital?
5. How long should Mark rest?
6. What did Mark decide to do while his ankle healed?

## Текст 3: A Marathon Runner's Story (История бегуна-марафонца)

Elena has been an athlete since she was a teenager, but she has never considered herself a professional. She first ran a half-marathon five years ago, and since then she has run four full marathons. "I have never won a race," she says with a smile, "but that's not why I run."

For Elena, running is a way to stay healthy and clear her mind. She trains five times a week, usually early in the morning before work. She always warms up for ten minutes and stretches afterwards. "You should listen to your body," she advises. "If you feel pain, you should stop and rest."

Of course, she has had some problems. Last winter she broke her finger during a fall, and once she had a bad cold that stopped her training for a month. But Elena believes that small setbacks are normal. "Have you ever felt like giving up?" a reporter asked her. "Sure," she answered. "But I've learnt that persistence is more important than talent."

Now Elena helps beginners in her local running club. She teaches them how to breathe correctly and how to pace themselves. "Have a plan, but be flexible. And remember: everyone who finishes a marathon is a winner."

### Вопросы к тексту 3

1. When did Elena run her first half-marathon?
2. How many full marathons has she run?
3. Why does Elena run?
4. What does she advise if you feel pain?
5. What injury did Elena have last winter?
6. According to Elena, what is more important than talent?
7. What does Elena teach to beginners?

## Итоговые вопросы на размышление (доступны ответы)

1. How many minutes does Anna walk in the described week? *(90; this is her routine, not a universal health threshold.)*
2. Does Anna’s bedtime prove her exact sleep duration? *(No. Time in bed and measured time asleep are different.)*
3. Who helped Mark in the fictional injury episode? *(Tom and a doctor; the episode does not define one treatment for every injury.)*
4. Is Elena’s statement about persistence a proven ranking of causes for everyone? *(No. It is the character’s view.)*

## Уточнение расширенного текста о привычках

Убраны универсальные утверждения «90 минут достаточно», «два литра воды для всех» и причинный вывод о пропуске завтрака. Теперь вопросы проверяют сведения личного распорядка. Для сопоставления исходных числовых советов использованы [NHS: физическая активность взрослых 19–64 лет](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/physical-activity-guidelines-for-adults-aged-19-to-64/) и [NHS: жидкость и гидратация](https://www.nhs.uk/Live-well/eat-well/food-guidelines-and-food-labels/water-drinks-nutrition/). Учебная история не заменяет эти рекомендации.
