# Unit 15 — 4 финальных диалога-ролевые ситуации с разбором

Выполняй диалоги в парах или вслух сам с собой (запись голоса на телефон).
Каждый диалог повторяет лексику и грамматику блоков 1–14.
После каждого диалога — разбор: полезные слова, грамматика, обороты для экзамена.

---

## Диалог 1. Собеседование на английском (Job Interview) — блоки 10, 04, 08

**Темы:** работа и профессии, описание людей, время и расписание.

> **Interviewer (HR):** Good morning, Anna. Please, make yourself comfortable. Thank you for coming.
>
> **Anna:** Good morning! Thank you for inviting me. I'm glad to be here.
>
> **Interviewer:** So, could you tell us a little about yourself?
>
> **Anna:** Of course. My name is Anna Petrova. I'm 27, and I live in Minsk. I graduated from the university with a degree in marketing, and for the last five years I have worked as a marketing specialist in an IT company.
>
> **Interviewer:** Have you ever led a team?
>
> **Anna:** Yes. Last year I managed a group of three people on a big advertising project. I think the most important thing is communication — we had daily short meetings and always met our deadlines.
>
> **Interviewer:** That's impressive. And what are your main strengths?
>
> **Anna:** I would say I am responsible, punctual and good at problem-solving. When something goes wrong, I stay calm and look for a solution instead of panicking.
>
> **Interviewer:** What about your weaknesses? I know it's a tricky question.
>
> **Anna:** (laughs) To be honest, I sometimes try to do too much myself instead of delegating. I'm working on it — I've learned that asking for help is not a weakness.
>
> **Interviewer:** Good answer. And why do you want to work for our company?
>
> **Anna:** Because your projects are international, and I would like to use my English every day. Also, your values match mine: innovation, honesty and teamwork.
>
> **Interviewer:** What salary are you expecting?
>
> **Anna:** I'd be happy with a salary of about 3,000 dollars per month, but I'm flexible. I care more about the opportunity to grow.
>
> **Interviewer:** One more thing — when can you start?
>
> **Anna:** I can start in two weeks. I just need to give notice to my current employer.
>
> **Interviewer:** Perfect. We'll contact you by Friday. Thank you, Anna.
>
> **Anna:** Thank you very much! It was a pleasure meeting you. Have a nice day!

### Разбор диалога 1

| Что посмотреть | Детали |
|----------------|--------|
| Идиомы собеседования | make yourself comfortable — располагайтесь; give notice — предупредить об уходе; meet the deadline — уложиться в срок |
| Present Perfect | I have worked (для опыта и с for/since) |
| Модальные | would like to, can, could (вежливо), should |
| Отвечаем на слабые места | «слабость» + план её исправления (I'm working on it) |
| Гибкие вопросы | salary expectations, why our company, when can you start |

**Твоя очередь:** ответь вслух на вопросы интервьюера о себе, своих навыках и целях.
Запиши ответ — 2 минуты. Послушай и отметь 2 ошибки.

## Диалог 2. Регистрация в отеле за рубежом (Hotel Check-in) — блоки 11, 03, 07

**Темы:** путешествия, числа и деньги, город и транспорт.

> **Anna:** Good evening! I have a reservation under the name Petrova.
>
> **Receptionist:** Let me check... Yes, Mrs Petrova, a double room with a sea view for three nights. Is that right?
>
> **Anna:** Exactly. We're probably going to arrive late tonight, but here we are — earlier than expected.
>
> **Receptionist:** No problem at all. Could I see your passports, please?
>
> **Anna:** Here you are. And here are both passports.
>
> **Receptionist:** Thank you. Just a moment... Your room is 407 on the fourth floor. The lift is to your right.
>
> **Anna:** Great. Is breakfast included in the price?
>
> **Receptionist:** Yes, breakfast is served from 7 to 10 in the restaurant on the ground floor. Would you like a wake-up call?
>
> **Anna:** No, thanks. We'll manage. One more question: can we pay by card?
>
> **Receptionist:** Of course. We'll process the payment at check-out. Here are your keys and the map of the hotel. Wi-Fi password is on the card.
>
> **Anna:** Wonderful. And what time is check-out?
>
> **Receptionist:** Check-out is at noon. Is there anything else I can help you with?
>
> **Anna:** Actually, yes. Could you recommend a good restaurant nearby? We'd like typical local food.
>
> **Receptionist:** Try "The Old Harbour" — ten minutes by taxi from here. The seafood there is excellent, but book a table early; it's always packed.
>
> **Anna:** Thank you for your help!
>
> **Receptionist:** You're welcome. Enjoy your stay!

### Разбор диалога 2

| Что посмотреть | Детали |
|----------------|--------|
| Вежливые просьбы | Could you ...? I'd like ...; Would you like ...? |
| Пассивный залог | Breakfast is served; the payment will be processed |
| Числа и этажи | Room 407, fourth floor, ground floor (британск.: 1-й этаж), noon (12:00) |
| Будущее время | we're going to ...; check-out is at noon (расписание — Present Simple) |
| Фразы ресепшена | Here you are; Just a moment; Enjoy your stay |

**Твоя очередь:** разыграй заселение с вежливой просьбой (получить номер с видом на город,
позвать горничную, продлить на одну ночь). Составь 6 реплик.

## Диалог 3. Разговор с врачом (Visiting the Doctor) — блоки 09, 05, 04

**Темы:** здоровье и спорт, еда и диета, семья.

> **Doctor:** Good morning! Please, sit down. What seems to be the problem?
>
> **Anna:** Good morning, doctor. I've had a headache and a sore throat since yesterday morning. Also, my temperature is 38.2.
>
> **Doctor:** I see. Did you take any medicine?
>
> **Anna:** Yes, I took a painkiller last night, but it didn't help much.
>
> **Doctor:** Hmm. How long have you had the cough?
>
> **Anna:** I don't have a cough, but my nose is blocked and I feel very weak.
>
> **Doctor:** Open your mouth, please... Your throat is a little red. Let me check your temperature... Yes, still elevated. When did it all start?
>
> **Anna:** After the weekend, actually. I think I caught a cold when I was walking in the rain without an umbrella.
>
> **Doctor:** Classic story. Well, it looks like a viral infection, not the flu. I'll prescribe some drops and an anti-inflammatory medicine. Take the medicine twice a day after meals.
>
> **Anna:** OK. Should I stay in bed?
>
> **Doctor:** Yes, rest is very important. Drink plenty of warm drinks — tea with lemon, for example — and avoid spicy food. And don't go to work until the temperature is normal for two days, otherwise you may infect others.
>
> **Anna:** Understood. How soon will I feel better?
>
> **Doctor:** Usually people recover in three to five days. If it doesn't get better by Friday, come back and we'll do some tests. Here is the prescription.
>
> **Anna:** Thank you very much, doctor!
>
> **Doctor:** Get well soon! Take care.

### Разбор диалога 3

| Что посмотреть | Детали |
|----------------|--------|
| Жалобы на здоровье | I've had a headache; my nose is blocked; I feel weak |
| Present Perfect | for длительности состояния: How long have you had ...? |
| Modal advice | should — совет; may — возможность; avoid + V-ing |
| Советы врача | take twice a day, stay in bed, drink plenty of ... |
| Слова врача | prescribe (выписать рецепт), prescription, recover (выздороветь), infection |

**Твоя очередь:** сыграй приём у врача: у пациента болит спина / высокая температура /
аллергия. Врач даёт 3 совета. Используй Past Simple для рассказа, когда началось.

## Диалог 4. Интервью для travel-влога (Travel Vlog Interview) — блоки 11, 12, 14

**Темы:** путешествия и туризм, культура, история и география.

> **Blogger:** Hi, everyone! I'm Jake, and today we're in beautiful Edinburgh. I have a guest — Anna from Belarus. Anna, is this your first time in Scotland?
>
> **Anna:** Hello, Jake! Yes, it is, and I'm absolutely amazed. Edinburgh is the most beautiful city I have ever visited.
>
> **Blogger:** That's amazing to hear! What impressed you the most?
>
> **Anna:** First of all, the castle. When you walk up the Royal Mile and see the castle on the top of the rock, it's like going back in time. And, of course, the views of the sea from the hill on Arthur's Seat.
>
> **Blogger:** Did you manage to try any local food?
>
> **Anna:** I did — I tried haggis and whisky in one traditional pub. Honestly, I was a bit nervous about the haggis, but it was really tasty. Your weather, though... one day we had rain, wind and sunshine all in one hour!
>
> **Blogger:** (laughs) Welcome to Scotland! What about the festival? Did you know that Edinburgh Festival is one of the biggest in the world?
>
> **Anna:** I saw posters everywhere. Unfortunately, we're visiting before the festival season, so I didn't go. But that's a perfect reason to come back next August!
>
> **Blogger:** Absolutely! And what did you learn about Scottish history?
>
> **Anna:** A lot! I didn't know that Scotland has its own system of law and education, and that the famous writer Robert Burns wrote in Scottish dialect. Also, the story of Mary, Queen of Scots, is like a real TV drama.
>
> **Blogger:** Great summary! Are you planning to visit any other places in the UK or Europe?
>
> **Anna:** Next week we're heading to London, then Paris. If I like them as much as Edinburgh, I might never go home! (laughs)
>
> **Blogger:** Ha! Let's hope you do. Thank you, Anna, and safe travels!
>
> **Anna:** Thank you, Jake! It was a pleasure talking to you.

### Разбор диалога 4

| Что посмотреть | Детали |
|----------------|--------|
| Superlatives | the most beautiful, the biggest |
| Present Perfect для опыта | the most beautiful I have ever visited; Have you ever ...? |
| Past Simple для рассказа | I tried, I saw, I didn't know |
| Условные предложения | If I like them ... I might never go home (1 тип) |
| Связки рассказа | first of all, unfortunately, actually, of course |
| Числа/история | Royal Mile, Arthur's Seat, haggis, whisky — культурные реалии |

**Твоя очередь:** дай интервью в роли туриста в своём родном городе. Ответь блогеру на 5 вопросов
(что понравилось, местная еда, погода, история, планы). Запиши себя на 1 минуту.

## Чек-лист готовности к устному экзамену

Пройди по пунктам перед итоговым тестом (test.md):

| Навык | Проверка |
|-------|----------|
| Представление | Расскажи о себе за 60 секунд (имя, город, семья, работа/учёба) |
| Описание | Опиши свой дом / город за 4 предложения |
| Еда | Закажи обед и попроси счёт — без запинки |
| Направления | Объясни, как дойти от метро до дома |
| Планы | Скажи, что будешь делать в выходные (going to) |
| Опыт | Расскажи о лучшей поездке (Present Perfect + Past Simple) |
| Здоровье | Опиши недомогание врачу |
| Мнение | Выскажи мнение: технологии — это хорошо или плохо? Почему? |

Если 6+ пунктов даются свободно — ты готов к экзамену. Если меньше — повтори блоки practice.md и диалоги выше.
