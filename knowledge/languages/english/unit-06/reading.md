## Текст 1: The Four Seasons

In many parts of the world, there are four seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Each season brings different weather and different activities.

Spring is a beautiful season. The weather is getting warmer. The flowers are starting to bloom. The trees are getting green leaves. Many animals wake up from their winter sleep. People enjoy being outside after the long winter.

Summer is the hottest season. The days are long and the sun shines brightly. Children don't go to school. Families go on vacation. They go to the beach or to the mountains. Many people like to swim in the sea or in the pool.

Autumn is the season of change. The leaves on the trees turn yellow, orange, and red. Then they fall to the ground. The weather becomes cooler. The days become shorter. Farmers harvest their crops. Birds fly to warmer places.

Winter is the coldest season. In some places, it snows a lot. The temperature is below zero. Lakes and rivers freeze. People wear warm clothes. Some people like to ski or snowboard. Others stay at home and drink hot tea by the fire.

Every season is special in its own way. People have different favorite seasons, but all of them are part of the beautiful cycle of nature.

2. What happens in spring?
3. What is the weather like in summer?
4. What happens to the leaves in autumn?
5. What do people do in winter?
6. What do you like to do in each season?

## Текст 2: Weather Around the World

Weather is very different around the world. In some countries, it is hot and sunny all year. In other places, there are four distinct seasons.

In Russia, the winters are long and cold. The temperature often drops below zero. Snow covers the ground for many months. In Siberia, it can be incredibly cold, sometimes minus fifty degrees! But Russian summers can be quite warm, especially in the south.

In Great Britain, the weather is famous for being changeable. It can rain in the morning, be sunny in the afternoon, and become cloudy in the evening. Because of this, British people always carry umbrellas. They often say: "If you don't like the weather, just wait a few minutes."

In the tropics, like in Brazil or Thailand, the climate is hot and humid. There are two main seasons: the rainy season and the dry season. During the rainy season, there are heavy showers almost every afternoon. During the dry season, the sun shines and it is very hot.

In the desert, like the Sahara, there is almost no rain. Days are extremely hot and nights are very cold because there are no clouds to keep the warmth. This is why deserts are so dry.

Every place has its own weather. That is why weather forecasts are so important for people all over the world. They help us plan our days and our journeys.

### Вопросы к тексту:
1. Why do British people always carry umbrellas?
2. What are the two main seasons in the tropics?
3. Why are desert nights cold?
4. How cold can Siberia be in winter?
5. What do weather forecasts help people to do?

## Текст 3: The Deep Forest

Last weekend I went on a trip to a deep forest in the north of the country. I wanted to see the wild nature and breathe the fresh air away from the busy city.

The forest was amazing. Tall pine trees stood everywhere, and the ground was covered with soft green moss. The air smelled of pine and wet earth. I could hear the wind moving through the branches and the birds singing high above me.

I walked along a small stream that ran through the middle of the forest. The water was so clear that I could see the pebbles at the bottom. A little waterfall made a quiet sound as it fell into a small pool.

In the afternoon, the weather changed. Dark clouds came quickly, and soon it started to rain. I found shelter under a big tree and watched the rain. The raindrops made circles on the surface of the pool. A deer came out of the trees and drank water from the stream. It was a magical moment.

The rain stopped after an hour. The sun appeared again, and a beautiful rainbow crossed the sky. I was wet and tired, but I felt very happy. The forest is a wonderful place, and I will never forget my trip there.

6. Would you like to visit a deep forest? Why?

## Текст 4: The Power of the Ocean

The ocean covers more than seventy percent of our planet. It is where most of the Earth's water lives, and it is full of amazing creatures.

There are many kinds of animals in the ocean: fish, whales, dolphins, sharks, crabs, and turtles. Whales are the largest animals in the world. A blue whale can be longer than a bus. Dolphins are very friendly and intelligent. They often swim near ships and play in the waves.

The ocean also has a huge influence on our weather. Ocean currents carry warm and cold water around the world. This affects the temperature and the rain in many countries. For example, the Gulf Stream makes the climate in Western Europe warmer than it would be otherwise.

But the ocean can be dangerous too. Hurricanes are born over warm ocean water. They bring strong winds, heavy rain, and huge waves to the coast. In winter, storms at sea are common, and sailors must be careful.

We need to protect the ocean. Pollution and plastic are big problems for sea animals. If we take care of the ocean, it will continue to give us food, oxygen, and beauty for many years.

1. Where does most of the Earth's water live?
2. Which animal is the largest in the world?
3. How does the ocean influence the weather?
4. Where are hurricanes born?
5. Why do we need to protect the ocean?
