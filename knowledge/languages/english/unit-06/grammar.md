# Unit 06 — Grammar (A2-B1 Level)

### 6.1. Weather Expressions

| Pattern | Example | Russian |
|---------|---------|---------|
| It is + adjective | It is sunny. | Солнечно. |
| It is + verb-ing | It is raining. | Идёт дождь. |
| There is + noun | There is a storm. | Шторм. |
| The temperature is... | The temperature is 25 degrees. | Температура 25 градусов. |

### 6.2. The Present Continuous for Weather

We use the Present Continuous to describe what the weather is doing **right now**.

| Sentence | Russian |
|----------|---------|
| It is raining. | Идёт дождь. |
| It is snowing. | Идёт снег. |
| The sun is shining. | Солнце светит. |
| The wind is blowing. | Дует ветер. |
| The rain is falling. | Идёт дождь (падает). |
| The clouds are moving. | Облака движутся. |

**Form:** subject + am/is/are + verb-ing.
- It **is raining** now.
- The birds **are singing**.

### 6.3. Present Continuous vs Present Simple

| Tense | Use | Example |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Present Simple | Regular actions, facts | It often rains in autumn. |
| Present Continuous | Temporary situations, current state | It is raining now. |

### Time Markers

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
|----------------|-------------------|
| always, often, usually, sometimes, never, every day, on Sundays, in summer | now, right now, at the moment, currently, today, this week, look!, listen! |

**Examples:**
- It **always rains** in November. (обычно)
- It **is raining** at the moment. (сейчас)

### 6.4. The structure "There is / There are"

We use **there is / there are** to say that something exists.

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| There is + singular noun | один предмет | There is a lake in the forest. |
| There are + plural noun | несколько предметов | There are many trees here. |
| There is + uncountable noun | неисчисляемые | There is snow on the ground. |

**Negative and Questions:**
- There **isn't** a river near the village.
- There **aren't** any bears here.
- **Is there** a beach nearby? — Yes, there is.
- **Are there** any wild animals? — No, there aren't.

**Note:** With "there is + a/an" we use the article. "There is a mountain." Not "there is mountain."

### 6.5. Comparatives and Superlatives

We use comparatives to compare two things and superlatives to compare three or more.

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| hot | hotter | the hottest |
| cold | colder | the coldest |
| warm | warmer | the warmest |
| long | longer | the longest |
| wide | wider | the widest |
| beautiful | more beautiful | the most beautiful |
| good | better | the best |
| bad | worse | the worst |

**Examples:**
- Summer is **hotter than** spring.
- Winter is **the coldest** season.
- The Volga is **longer than** the Dnieper.
- Autumn is **more beautiful than** summer, in my opinion.
- Mount Everest is **the highest** mountain in the world.

### 6.6. Prepositions of Time with Seasons

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| in | months, seasons, years | in summer, in July, in 2026 |
| in (the) | periods of day | in the morning, in the afternoon |
| on | specific days, dates | on Monday, on 5 May, on a hot day |
| at | specific times, night | at noon, at night, at 5 o'clock |
| during | within a period | during the winter, during the storm |
| until | up to a time | until spring, until next month |

**Examples:**
- I love walking **in** spring.
- The snow falls **in** winter.
- We worked **on** a rainy day.
- The temperature drops **at** night.
- The lake freezes **during** the cold months.

### 6.7. "Will" for Weather Forecasts

We use **will** to make predictions about the future weather.

| Form | Example | Russian |
|------|---------|---------|
| will + verb | It will rain tomorrow. | Завтра будет дождь. |
| won't + verb | It won't snow this week. | На этой неделе снега не будет. |
| Will...? | Will it be sunny on Sunday? | Будет ли солнечно в воскресенье? |

**Examples:**
- The forecast says it **will be** sunny tomorrow.
- It **will rain** in the afternoon.
- It **won't be** cold tonight.
- **Will** there **be** a storm? — Yes, there will.

### 6.8. Adverbs of Frequency with Weather

| Adverb | Frequency | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| always | 100% | It is always hot in July. |
| usually | ~80% | It usually rains here in autumn. |
| often | ~70% | It often snows in January. |
| sometimes | ~50% | The sky is sometimes cloudy. |
| rarely | ~10% | It rarely snows in the south. |
| never | 0% | It never snows in the desert. |

### 6.9. Prepositions of Place (recap)

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| in | inside, within | in the box, in the forest |
| on | on the surface | on the table, on the hill |
| at | specific point | at the river, at the station |
| under | below | under the tree |
| above | higher | above the clouds |
| between | between two things | between two mountains |
| among | among many things | among the trees |
| in front of | ahead | in front of the house |
| behind | at the back | behind the mountain |
| next to / beside | by the side | next to the lake |
| near / close to | not far | near the river |
| far from | distant | far from the city |

### 6.10. Describing Nature

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| a ___ mountain | a high/tall mountain |
| a ___ river | a long/wide river |
| a ___ forest | a dense/beautiful forest |
| the weather is ___ | the weather is nice/awful |
| the sky is ___ | the sky is clear/blue/gray |
| the air is ___ | the air is fresh/cold |
| it is + adj | it is humid/windy |
| the ___ is full of ... | the forest is full of animals |

**Formation of weather adjectives from nouns:**
- cloud → cloudy
- rain → rainy
- sun → sunny
- snow → snowy
- wind → windy
- fog → foggy
- storm → stormy

### 6.11. "What...like?" Questions

We ask **What is the weather like?** (not "How is the weather?" — though both are used informally).

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What's the weather like today? | It's sunny and warm. |
| What was the weather like yesterday? | It was cold and rainy. |
| What will the weather be like tomorrow? | It will be cloudy. |
| What's the temperature like? | It is around 15 degrees. |

| Idiom | Meaning | Example |
