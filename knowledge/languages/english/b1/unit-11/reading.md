# Английский · Урок 11 — чтение: Поездка: фон и последовательность событий

## Перед чтением

Прочитайте вымышленную ситуацию без перевода. Для ответа найдите строку-доказательство; отделяйте факт от плана, мнения и отсутствующих данных.

## Короткий текст

Last Saturday, Leila visited a transport museum with Omar. At nine, she was waiting at the bus stop when the bus arrived. She dropped her ticket while she was looking for her phone. The driver waited, and she found the ticket under her bag. Omar was already on the bus. They reached the museum at ten. While they were looking at an old map, a guide offered to show them a model train. They accepted. Neither of them lost a phone during the visit.

## Опорные слова

| Выражение | Значение |
|---|---|
| was waiting | ждала в рассматриваемый момент |
| dropped | уронила |
| reached | добрались до |
| offered | предложил |
| accepted | согласились, приняли предложение |

## Вопросы до ключа

1. What was Leila doing when the bus arrived?
2. What did she drop, and where did she find it?
3. Was Omar waiting outside the bus at that moment?
4. What did the guide offer while they were looking at the map?

<details>
<summary>Объяснённые ответы</summary>

1. She was waiting at the bus stop. Ожидание представлено как процесс в момент прибытия автобуса.
2. Her ticket; under her bag. Поиск телефона не означает, что потерян именно телефон.
3. No. He was already on the bus. Не переносите действие Лейлы на Омара.
4. To show them a model train. They accepted. Предложение произошло на фоне рассматривания карты; это не значит, что Past Simple всегда описывает физически короткое действие.

</details>

Омар уже был в автобусе. В десять они добрались до музея. Пока они рассматривали старую карту, экскурсовод предложил показать им модель поезда. Они согласились. Во время посещения никто из них не потерял телефон.

## Текст 1: My First Trip to London

Last summer I went to London for the first time. I flew from Moscow early in the morning. The flight took about four hours, and I slept most of the way.

When I arrived at Heathrow Airport, I passed through passport control and got my luggage. Then I took the train to the city centre and found my hotel without any problems.

In the afternoon I went sightseeing. I visited the Tower of London and walked across Tower Bridge. The next day I explored the British Museum — it is free, and it is huge! In the evening I wandered around the old streets and took lots of photos.

I spent four days in London. I didn't visit everything, of course, but I really enjoyed the trip. It was a wonderful experience, and I would love to go back one day.

### Лексика текста

| Word | Перевод |
|------|---------|
| for the first time | впервые |
| most of the way | бо́льшую часть пути |
| passed through passport control | прошёл паспортный контроль |
| found (find) | нашёл (находить) |
| without any problems | без всяких проблем |
| huge | огромный |
| wandered around (wander) | бродил по (бродить) |
| took lots of photos | сделал много фотографий |
| spent (spend) | потратил, провёл (тратить) |
| to go back one day | вернуться когда-нибудь |

### Вопросы к тексту

1. When did the writer go to London? (Last summer.)
2. How did she/he get to the city centre? (By train.)
3. Which museums did she/he visit? (The British Museum.)
4. Was the museum visit paid or free? (It was free.)
5. How many days did she/he spend in London? (Four days.)
6. Did she/he enjoy the trip? (Yes, she/he really enjoyed it.)

## Текст 2: How to Plan a Trip Abroad — Eight Tips

Planning a trip abroad is not difficult if you follow a few simple rules. Here is some practical advice.

First, check your passport. Is it valid for at least six months after your return? Some countries do not let you enter if your passport is almost expired. Second, find out whether you need a visa. Some nations allow visa-free travel, but others don't.

Then book your flight and hotel in advance. Tickets are cheaper if you buy them early. When you book a hotel, ask if breakfast is included and whether the room has air conditioning. Read reviews from other travellers before you pay.

Don't forget travel insurance. Medical care can be very expensive abroad, so insurance is a must. Print or download your tickets and insurance documents — keep copies in your bag too.

Pack light. Take only the things you really need. A backpack is better than three heavy suitcases. Put all important documents in your hand luggage, never in checked baggage.

Finally, arrive at the airport early — usually two or three hours before departure. Check-in and security checks take time, especially in summer. If you are calm and well-prepared, your trip will be a good experience.

### Лексика текста

| Word | Перевод |
|------|---------|
| plan (v) | планировать |
| advice | совет (неисчисляемое: advice, не advices) |
| valid | действительный (о документе) |
| for at least six months | как минимум на шесть месяцев |
| after your return | после вашего возвращения |
| almost expired | почти истёкший (о сроке) |
| find out | выяснить |
| visa-free travel | безвизовый въезд |
| in advance | заранее |
| air conditioning | кондиционер |
| reviews | отзывы |
| insurance is a must | страховка обязательна |
| keep copies | храните копии |
| pack light | собирайтесь налегке |
| well-prepared | хорошо подготовленный |

### Вопросы к тексту

1. Why does the author advise checking your passport first? (It must be valid after your return, otherwise some countries won't let you enter.)
2. How can you save money on tickets? (Buy them early / in advance.)
3. What should you ask about when booking a hotel? (Whether breakfast is included and whether the room has air conditioning.)
4. Why is travel insurance described as "a must"? (Medical care abroad can be very expensive.)
5. Where should you keep your important documents? (In your hand luggage, never in checked baggage.)
6. How early should you arrive at the airport? (Two or three hours before departure.)
7. Does the author advise taking many suitcases? (No, she/he advises packing light.)

## Текст 3: Have You Ever Been to Paris?

"Have you ever been to Paris?" my friend Anna asked me. "No, I've never been," I answered. "But I've always wanted to see the Eiffel Tower."

Last spring I decided to make my dream come true. I booked a cheap flight and a small hotel near the Seine. I planned everything — but, as usual, something went wrong.

My flight was delayed for three hours. When I finally landed, I was tired and hungry. I took a taxi to the city centre, checked in at the hotel and went straight to sleep.

The next morning everything changed. I woke up early, had a croissant for breakfast and walked to Notre-Dame. I climbed to the top and looked at the whole city. It was unforgettable.

During the following days I visited the Louvre, went on a boat trip along the river and sat in little cafés. In the evening I watched the Eiffel Tower light up — millions of tiny lights sparkling above me.

Now, when people ask me, "Have you ever been to Paris?", I always answer, "Yes, I have, and I will definitely go again!"

### Лексика текста

| Word | Перевод |
|------|---------|
| make my dream come true | осуществить мою мечту |
| as usual | как обычно |
| something went wrong | что-то пошло не так |
| landed (land) | приземлился (приземляться) |
| went straight to sleep | сразу лёг спать |
| unforgettable | незабываемый |
| lit up (light up) | подсветился (загореться огнями) |
| millions of tiny lights | миллионы крошечных огоньков |
| sparkling | сверкающие |
| definitely | точно, однозначно |

### Вопросы к тексту

1. Had the writer been to Paris before this trip? (No, he/she had never been.)
2. Why was he/she tired when he/she arrived? (The flight was delayed for three hours.)
3. What did he/she do after checking in at the hotel? (Went straight to sleep.)
4. Which three sights did he/she visit? (Notre-Dame, the Louvre, the Eiffel Tower.)
5. What did he/she watch in the evening? (The Eiffel Tower lighting up.)
6. What is the writer's answer to "Have you ever been to Paris?" now? (Yes, I have, and I will definitely go again.)
7. Ничего не вышло? Подсказка: слово из лексики — unforgettable. (Свободный вопрос: чем запомнилась поездка? Ответ может быть любым: e.g. It was unforgettable because of the Eiffel Tower.)
