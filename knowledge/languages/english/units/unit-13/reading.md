## Текст 1: История интернета (The History of the Internet)

The Internet is a global network that connects billions of devices. It all started in the 1960s, when scientists in the USA created a network called ARPANET. At first, it was used by universities and the military. Researchers used it to share data and files.

In the 1980s, the Internet became more useful. People could send e-mails and join online forums. However, using it was still difficult because there were no modern browsers.

The real revolution came in 1991, when the World Wide Web was created by Tim Berners-Lee. Suddenly, ordinary people could access websites with just one click. Search engines like Google made it easier to find information.

Today, the Internet is everywhere. We use it for work, shopping, education, and communication. Video calls connect people who are thousands of kilometres apart. Social media lets us share our lives with friends.

But the Internet also has a dark side. Hackers can steal personal data, and misinformation spreads fast online. That is why digital literacy and online security are so important in the modern world.

2. Who used the Internet at first?
3. Who created the World Wide Web and when?
4. What changed in 1991?
5. What is the "dark side" of the Internet mentioned in the text?
6. Why is digital literacy important nowadays?

## Текст 2: Кибербезопасность (Cybersecurity)

Every day, thousands of people become victims of cybercrime. Hackers use different tricks to steal passwords, money, and personal information. One common method is phishing: criminals send fake e-mails that look real and ask you to enter your password or credit card number.

Another dangerous thing is malware. Malware can be hidden in downloaded files, in fake apps, or even in attachments. Once a virus is installed, it can delete files, spy on you, or use your computer to attack other people.

How can you protect yourself? First, you must create strong passwords and use a different one for every account. You should never reuse the same password everywhere. Second, always update your software, because updates fix security bugs.

Third, if an e-mail looks suspicious, don't click on any links in it. Check the sender's address carefully. And remember: if something sounds too good to be true, it probably is a scam.

Finally, it is very important to back up your files. If your data is stolen or deleted, you will still have copies. Cybersecurity is not about being paranoid; it is about being careful with your digital life.

3. Why should you use a different password for every account?
4. What should you do if an e-mail looks suspicious?
5. Why is it important to back up your files?
6. What is the main idea of the last sentence?

## Текст 3: Жизнь без телефона (Life Without a Phone)

Last month, I decided to try a digital detox. I was tired of checking my phone every five minutes, so I put it in a drawer for a whole weekend and switched it off.

The first day was strange. In the morning, I reached for my phone on the nightstand, but it wasn't there. I felt lost. Normally, I scrolled through social media during breakfast and read the news in bed. Now I had nothing to do with my hands.

Then I started to notice things. I looked out of the window and saw how green the trees were. I made a real coffee instead of just ordering food online. I talked to my family face to face for hours. We played board games and laughed a lot.

At first, it was difficult, but by Sunday evening I felt calm and free. I found out that I had much more free time than I thought. I read two books in two days!

On Monday, I turned on my phone again. Hundreds of notifications appeared on the screen. But none of them were really urgent. I realised that I don't need to be online all the time. Now I limit my screen time to two hours a day, and I feel much better.
