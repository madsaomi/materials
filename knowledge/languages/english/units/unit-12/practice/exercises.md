# Unit 12 — Practice (B1/B2 Level)

13 упражнений на лексику и грамматику блока «Культура и искусство». Ответы — после каждого упражнения. Сначала сделай сам(а), потом проверяй.

## Упражнение 1. Relative clauses — выбери who / which / where / whose

**Задание:** вставь подходящее слово: **who, which, where, whose**.

1. The artist ___ painted this picture died in 1910.
2. The film ___ won the award was a drama.
3. This is the theatre ___ we watched the ballet.
4. She is the singer ___ voice everyone loves.
5. The music ___ they play at the parade is very cheerful.
6. The museum ___ we met is famous for its sculptures.

**Ответы:** 1) who, 2) which, 3) where, 4) whose, 5) which, 6) where

---

## Упражнение 2. Relative clauses — можно ли опустить?

**Задание:** отметь ✓, где who/which/that можно опустить (объект), и ✗, где нельзя (подлежащее).

1. The film which we watched last night was long.
2. The actor who plays the king is my friend.
3. The song that she sang became famous.
4. The director who made this movie is very young.
5. The painting that hangs in the hall is called "Storm".

**Ответы:** 1) ✓ (we — подлежащее после which), 2) ✗ (plays — глагол), 3) ✓ (she), 4) ✗ (made — глагол), 5) ✗ (hangs — глагол)

## Упражнение 3. Артикли с неисчисляемыми

**Задание:** поставь a/an, the или — (нулевой артикль).

1. ___ art is part of human culture.
2. ___ music in this film is beautiful.
3. I play ___ violin and ___ guitar.
4. ___ literature teaches us about life.
5. This is ___ piece of ___ music for piano.
6. ___ arts include painting, music and theatre.

**Ответы:** 1) — , 2) The, 3) the, the (с инструментами — всегда the), 4) — , 5) a piece of — music (после a piece артикля нет: *a piece of music*), 6) The (the arts — множественное: виды искусств)

---

## Упражнение 4. Passive Voice — Present Simple

**Задание:** перепиши предложения в пассив по формуле am/is/are + V3.

1. The museum opens the gallery at nine. → The gallery ...
2. They sell tickets at the box office. → Tickets ...
3. People speak English here. → English ...
4. The director invites many actors. → Many actors ...

**Ответы:**

1. The gallery **is opened** at nine.
2. Tickets **are sold** at the box office.
3. English **is spoken** here.
4. Many actors **are invited** (by the director).

---

## Упражнение 5. Passive Voice — Past Simple

**Задание:** образуй пассив прошедшего времени (was/were + V3).

1. Shakespeare wrote "Hamlet". → "Hamlet" ... (write→written)
2. Alfred Hitchcock directed this thriller. → This thriller ... (direct→directed)
3. They made the film in 1995. → The film ... (make→made)
4. Two composers wrote the music. → The music ... (write→written)

**Ответы:**

1. "Hamlet" **was written** by Shakespeare.
2. This thriller **was directed** by Alfred Hitchcock.
3. The film **was made** in 1995.
4. The music **was written** by two composers.

---

## Упражнение 6. Дополни предложение пассивом

**Задание:** заполни пропуски, используя правильную форму пассива (Present или Past Simple).

1. Mona Lisa ___ (paint) by Leonardo da Vinci.
2. The exhibition ___ (hold) every summer.
3. These costumes ___ (make) by local artists last year.
4. The play ___ (perform) by the school theatre group tomorrow?

**Ответы:**

1. was painted
2. is held
3. were made
4. Will the play be performed...? ♣ (бонус: future passive will be performed; правильный ответ: Will the play be performed...)

> ♣ В 4-м предложении пассив в будущем времени: **Will the play be performed** by the school theatre group tomorrow? Если ты ещё не прошёл(а) will be + V3 — загляни в grammar.md §8.

## Упражнение 7. Present Perfect Continuous

**Задание:** образуй Present Perfect Continuous (have/has been + V-ing).

1. She ___ (learn) to play the guitar for three years.
2. They ___ (rehearse) the play since morning.
3. How long ___ you ___ (watch) this documentary?
4. We ___ (visit) galleries every weekend since 2022.
5. He ___ (write) his novel all day — he needs a break.

**Ответы:**

1. has been learning
2. have been rehearsing
3. How long have you been watching...?
4. have been visiting
5. has been writing

## Упражнение 8. like / love / hate / enjoy + -ing

**Задание:** раскрой скобки, поставив глагол в форму с -ing (или инфинитив с to там, где нужно).

1. I enjoy ___ (listen) to classical music.
2. She loves ___ (paint) portraits.
3. We like ___ (go) to the theatre on weekends.
4. I would like ___ (see) this opera, please.
5. He hates ___ (wait) for the show to start.
6. They enjoy ___ (watch) documentaries about art.

**Ответы:**

1. listening (enjoy + -ing)
2. painting (love + -ing)
3. going (like + -ing; допустимо like to go)
4. to see (would like + to — обязательно инфинитив!)
5. waiting (hate + -ing)
6. watching (enjoy + -ing)

## Упражнение 9. Сравнения: more ... than / as ... as

**Задание:** заполни пропуски: **more ... than, not as ... as, -er than**.

1. This film is ___ (interesting) the previous one.
2. This song is ___ (popular) that ancient lullaby — it's less known.
3. Modern art is ___ (complicated) simple painting.
4. The theatre is ___ (old) the cinema in this town.
5. This portrait is ___ (beautiful) the landscape, in my opinion.

**Ответы:**

1. more interesting than
2. not as popular as
3. more complicated than
4. older than
5. more beautiful than (допустимо: as beautiful as в контексте сравнения — но по заданию здесь most natural: more beautiful than)

## Упражнение 10. Превосходная степень

**Задание:** раскрой скобки в превосходной степени (the most ... / the + -est).

1. "Hamlet" is one of ___ (famous) plays in the world.
2. This is ___ (funny) comedy I have ever seen.
3. Venice hosts one of ___ (old) carnivals in Europe.
4. She is ___ (talented) artist in our class.
5. That was ___ (bad) performance of the season.

**Ответы:**

1. the most famous
2. the funniest
3. the oldest
4. the most talented
5. the worst

## Упражнение 13. Перевод с русского на английский

**Задание:** переведи предложения, используя слова и конструкции блока.

1. Фильм был снят молодым режиссёром. (was directed)
2. Моцарт написал эту оперу в 1786 году. (was written — пассив)
3. Я смотрю этот сериал с мая. (Present Perfect Continuous)
4. Она любит читать биографии. (loves reading)
5. Этот музей популярнее того. (more popular than)
6. Хэллоуин празднуют 31 октября. (is celebrated)

**Ответы:**

1. The film was directed by a young director.
2. The opera was written by Mozart in 1786.
3. I have been watching this series since May.
4. She loves reading biographies.
5. This museum is more popular than that one.
6. Halloween is celebrated on the 31st of October.
