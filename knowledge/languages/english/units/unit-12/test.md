# Unit 12 — Test (B1/B2 Level)

Тест по блоку «Культура и искусство»: 25 вопросов (лексика + грамматика). Запиши ответы, затем проверь по ключу внизу.

## Часть 1. Лексика — выбери правильный вариант (1–10)

1. What is a "painter"? A) тот, кто рисует картины B) сценарист C) дирижёр → ___
2. The long text of a film that the actors perform is the ... A) plot B) screenplay C) trailer → ___
3. Before the premiere the actors have a ... A) feast B) rehearsal C) parade → ___
4. "Шедевр" по-английски: A) bestseller B) nostalgia C) masterpiece → ___
5. The curtain goes up, and the ... begins. A) play B) author C) gallery → ___
6. A movie that continues a story from the first film is a ... A) genre B) sequel C) chapter → ___
7. Вера sings in a ... on Sundays. A) choir B) painting C) statue → ___
8. The museum has a special ... of ancient coins. A) exhibition B) illustration C) balance → ___
9. On Halloween children go from door to door and say "..." A) Goodnight B) Trick or treat! C) Bon voyage → ___
10. The singer performs at a big ... tonight. A) rehearsal B) concert C) ceremony → ___

## Часть 2. Грамматика — выбери правильный вариант (11–20)

11. The artist ___ painted this picture lived in Paris.
    A) who B) which C) where → ___

12. The museum ___ we saw the Rosetta Stone is in London.
    A) who B) where C) whose → ___

13. ___ art is a window into history.
    A) An B) The C) — → ___

14. ___ music in the film was composed by a famous musician.
    A) The B) A C) — → ___

15. "Hamlet" ___ by Shakespeare.
    A) was written B) wrote C) is writing → ___

16. Мона Лиза ___ written by da Vinci? — No, it ___ painted by him!
    A) was / is B) was / was C) is / was → ___

17. The exhibition ___ every summer.
    A) is held B) held C) has held → ___

18. She ___ the piano for ten years.
    A) is playing B) has been playing C) plays now → ___

19. I enjoy ___ to live concerts.
    A) go B) to go C) going → ___

20. This gallery is ___ popular ___ the National Museum.
    A) most / than B) more / than C) more / as → ___

## Часть 3. Развёрнутые задания (21–25)

21. Поставь слово в правильную форму: This is the ___ (funny) film I have ever seen.
22. Образуй пассив: Somebody wrote the novel in 1898. → The novel ...
23. Образуй пассив: They hold the festival in May. → The festival ...
24. Вставь предлог: This portrait was painted ___ van Gogh. The drawing was done ___ a pencil.
25. Переведи: «Я люблю смотреть документальные фильмы.» (используй love + -ing)
