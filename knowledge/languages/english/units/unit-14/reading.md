## Текст 1: The Making of the United States

In 1607 a small group of English settlers founded the first permanent colony at Jamestown, Virginia. Over the next century more colonies appeared along the Atlantic coast, and by 1775 there were thirteen British colonies in North America. The colonists, however, grew tired of British taxes and laws. They wanted to rule themselves.

The American Revolution began in 1775. On the 4th of July, 1776, the Declaration of Independence was signed, and the newly declared states were called the United States of America. Thomas Jefferson wrote the famous words: "All men are created equal." The war lasted until 1783, when Britain recognised the new country. In 1787 the Constitution was adopted, and George Washington became the first President in 1789.

Later, the young nation grew quickly. Pioneers moved west across the continent, and new states were added. In the 19th century millions of immigrants arrived from Europe. The USA became one of the largest and most powerful countries in the world. Today it has fifty states, a big population, and a huge influence on global politics and economics.

## Текст 2: London — History and Sights

London, the capital of the United Kingdom, stands on the river Thames and has a history of more than two thousand years. The Romans founded it as Londinium around 43 AD. In the Middle Ages London became a rich trading city, and later it grew into the centre of a world empire.

Some of the world's most famous landmarks are in London. The Tower of London was built by William the Conqueror in the 11th century. Big Ben, the great clock tower, was completed in 1859. St Paul's Cathedral with its huge dome was designed by Sir Christopher Wren after the Great Fire of 1666. The British Museum, which opened in 1759, is free to everyone and shows treasures from all over the world.

Today London is a huge and modern city. About nine million people live there. You can see people of almost every nationality, and more than three hundred languages are spoken. The city's history and its present-day energy make London one of the most visited capital cities on Earth.

## Текст 3: Australia — Land of Contrasts

Australia is the smallest continent and the largest island in the world. It lies in the southern hemisphere between the Indian and Pacific oceans. The country is famous for its huge empty areas called the Outback, which is mostly desert. Most Australians live along the coasts, where the climate is warmer and wetter.

The first Australians were the Aborigines, who came to the continent thousands of years ago. In 1770 the British explorer James Cook reached the east coast. In 1788 the first British colony was founded at Sydney Cove, and for many years it was a place to send convicts from Britain. Later, free settlers arrived, and some of them discovered gold in the 1850s. That discovery brought many new people to the country.

Today Australia is a rich, modern nation with friendly people. Its largest cities are Sydney, famous for the Opera House and the Harbour Bridge, and Melbourne, one of the world's liveliest cities. The capital, Canberra, is much smaller than Sydney and Melbourne. Australia is also known for unique animals such as kangaroos, koalas and the platypus, which live nowhere else in the world.

## Текст 4 (дополнительный): Canada — Geography in Numbers

Canada is the second largest country in the world by area, but its population is only about 38 million people. For comparison, the USA has more than 330 million inhabitants. This means Canada is very empty — most Canadians live close to the southern border with the USA.

The country is divided into ten provinces and three territories. The largest province is Ontario, where the capital Ottawa and the biggest city Toronto are situated. Huge forests cover the north, and the Rocky Mountains rise in the west. Canada has vast lakes: Lake Superior, Lake Huron and Lake Ontario are part of the Great Lakes system, which it shares with the USA.

Two official languages are spoken in Canada: English and French. Some cities, like Montreal, are mainly French-speaking. Canada is a member of the Commonwealth and is a close ally of the USA and the UK.
