## Упражнение 2: Past Simple или Past Continuous? (10 предложений)

1. While they ______ (cross) the desert, the storm ______ (begin).
→ were crossing / began
2. I ______ (read) about the Revolution when the phone ______ (ring).
→ was reading / rang
3. The settlers ______ (build) houses while women ______ (prepare) dinner.
→ built (быстрое) / were preparing — оба длительных: were building / were preparing
4. What ______ you ______ (do) at six o'clock yesterday evening? → were / doing
5. When the ship ______ (arrive), it ______ (start) to rain. → arrived / started
6. The guide ______ (speak) English while the tourists ______ (take) photos.
→ was speaking / were taking
7. History ______ (change) while people ______ (watch). → changed / were watching
8. They ______ (not listen) when the professor ______ (explain) the battle.
→ weren't listening / explained
9. ______ it ______ (rain) when Columbus ______ (reach) the island?
→ Was / raining / reached
10. The Vikings ______ (leave) before we ______ (arrive). → had left / arrived

## Упражнение 4: Активный → страдательный залог (8 предложений)

1. Britain founded the colony in 1788. → The colony was founded by Britain in 1788.
2. The Romans built the first London Bridge. → The first London Bridge was built by the Romans.
3. They signed the treaty in Paris. → The treaty was signed in Paris.
4. The pioneers discovered gold in the 1850s. → Gold was discovered by the pioneers in the 1850s.
5. Parliament approved the new law in 1832. → The new law was approved by Parliament in 1832.
6. The explorers didn't find the river. → The river wasn't found by the explorers.
7. Did they hold the summit in the capital? → Was the summit held in the capital?
8. Someone destroyed the old castle. → The old castle was destroyed.

## Упражнение 10: Перевод русский → английский (8 предложений)

1. Сидней стоит на восточном побережье Австралии. → Sydney is on the east coast of Australia.
2. Декларация независимости была подписана в 1776 году. → The Declaration of Independence was signed in 1776.
3. Канада больше, чем Австралия? → Is Canada bigger than Australia?
4. Темза длиннее, чем Миссисипи? Нет. → Is the Thames longer than the Mississippi? No.
5. Раньше страна была колонией Британии. → The country used to be a British colony.
6. Президент встретится с премьер-министром в столице. → The President will meet the Prime Minister in the capital.
7. Пока он читал о войне, звонил телефон. → While he was reading about the war, the phone rang.
8. Город был основан поселенцами в 1788 году. → The city was founded by settlers in 1788.

## Упражнение 13: Составьте предложения из слов (5 предложений)

1. the / was / USA / founded / 1776 / in → The USA was founded in 1776. (declared)
2. is / the / Australia / continent / smallest → Australia is the smallest continent.
3. capital / Wellington / the / New Zealand / of / is → Wellington is the capital of New Zealand.
