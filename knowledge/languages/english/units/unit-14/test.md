# Unit 14 — Test (B2 Level — История и география)

## Часть 1: Грамматика (10 вопросов)

1. Choose the correct form: The Declaration of Independence (was signed / signed) in 1776. → was signed
2. Choose the correct form: While they (travelled / were travelling) across the desert, the guide told stories. → were travelling
3. Choose the correct article: ___ Thames is a river in London. → the
4. Choose the correct article: Lake Baikal is in ___ Siberia (ничего / the). → ничего (—)
5. Choose the correct article: ___ USA has fifty states. → the
6. Choose the correct form: Australia is ___ (small) continent. → the smallest
7. Choose the correct form: Canada is ___ than the USA (large). → larger
8. Choose the correct quantifier: There is not ___ (much / many) rain in the desert. → much
9. Choose the correct form: The city ___ by pioneers in 1788. (A) was founded (B) founded (C) is founded → A
10. Choose the correct form: I ___ to live in London, but now I live in Edinburgh. (A) use (B) used (C) am used → B

## Часть 2: Словарный запас (8 вопросов)

13. What is «независимость»? (A) independence (B) treaty (C) election → A

15. Match: 1) mountain 2) island 3) valley 4) waterfall — a) водопад b) долина c) остров d) гора → 1–d, 2–c, 3–b, 4–a
16. Which word means «историческое событие в прошлом, которое прямо рассказывает о датах»? (A) history (B) century (C) decade → A
17. Choose the nationality: a person from Canada is a ___ . (A) Canadian (B) Canadian man (C) Canada → A
18. Which river flows through London? (A) the Mississippi (B) the Thames (C) the Murray → B
