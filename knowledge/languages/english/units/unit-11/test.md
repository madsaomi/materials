## Часть 1: Лексика (вопросы 1–10)

Выберите правильный вариант.

1. Что из этого предъявляют на границе?
   A) boarding pass → B) passport → C) room service → **B**

2. Где выдают чемоданы после прилёта?
   A) baggage claim → B) departure lounge → C) minibar → **A**

3. Слово для «экскурсовода»:
   A) pilot → B) guide → C) receptionist → **B**

4. The opposite of "departure" is:
   A) arrival → B) transit → C) check-in → **A**

5. «Ручная кладь» — это:
   A) checked baggage → B) luggage trolley → C) hand luggage → **C**

6. Что вы получите, если рейс перенесут на два часа?
   A) a delay → B) a landmark → C) a map → **A**

7. «Билет туда и обратно» по-английски:
   A) one-way ticket → B) return ticket → C) boarding pass → **B**

8. В каком месте отеля живут двое в комнате с двумя кроватями?
   A) twin room → B) single room → C) suite → **A**

9. Место, где осматривают достопримечательности, называется:
   A) sightseeing → B) customs → C) runway → **A**

10. «Завтрак включён в стоимость» — это «Breakfast is ___»:
    A) included → B) cancelled → C) booked → **A**

## Часть 2: Грамматика (вопросы 11–20)

Выберите правильный вариант.

11. Last year I ___ to Rome.
    A) gone → B) went → C) go → **B**

12. Have you ever ___ to the Colosseum?
    A) been → B) went → C) go → **A**

13. She ___ to London and she's still there.
    A) has been → B) has gone → C) went → **B**

14. We ___ our flight yesterday, sad but true.
    A) miss → B) missed → C) have missed → **B**

15. The train ___ Paris ___ Nice every morning.
    A) from... to → B) to... from → C) in... at → **A**

16. How ___ does it take to get to the airport?
    A) many → B) long → C) much → **B**

17. When we ___ the museum, it started to rain.
    A) were visiting → B) visited → C) have visited → **A** (фон + событие → Past Continuous + Past Simple; см. пояснение ниже)

18. They travelled ___ foot around the island.
    A) by → B) on → C) in → **B**

19. Could I ___ the bill, please?
    A) have → B) to have → C) having → **A**

20. You ___ book tickets in advance.
    A) should to → B) should → C) shouldn't ... → **B** (после should глагол без to)

*Пояснение к 17: допустим и вариант B, если действие единично и без фона. В этом тесте правильный ответ — A, так как описана ситуация «когда мы осматривали музей, начался дождь».*

## Часть 3: Чтение (вопросы 21–25)

Прочитайте текст и ответьте на вопросы.

> Stefan went to Barcelona on his birthday. He flew with a low-cost airline, so he only took hand luggage. At the hotel he asked for a room with a view, but all rooms with a view were booked. The hotel had a rooftop swimming pool, though, and Stefan loved it. Every morning he went for a swim before breakfast. On the last day, he bought a souvenir — a small ceramic dragon — and sent a postcard to his grandmother. He says he has never had a better trip.

21. When did Stefan go to Barcelona?
    A) at Christmas → B) on his birthday → C) in summer → **B**

22. Why did he take only hand luggage?
    A) He didn't have a suitcase → B) He flew with a low-cost airline → C) He forgot his bag → **B**

23. What did the hotel give him?
    A) a room with a view → B) a room with a balcony → C) a room without a view → **C** (все номера с видом были забронированы)

24. What did he do every morning?
    A) swam in the rooftop pool → B) went sightseeing → C) bought souvenirs → **A**

25. Which sentence is TRUE according to the text?
    A) It was his best trip ever → B) He was angry with the hotel → C) He sent a photo to his grandmother → **A**
