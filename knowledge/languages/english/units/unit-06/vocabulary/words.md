## 6.7. Useful Phrases

| English | Russian | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| What's the weather like? | Какая погода? | What's the weather like in Moscow? |
| It's sunny/cloudy/rainy. | Солнечно/облачно/дождливо. | It's cloudy today. |
| What's the temperature? | Какая температура? | What's the temperature now? |
| It's 25 degrees. | 25 градусов. | It's 25 degrees. |
| It looks like rain. | Похоже на дождь. | It looks like rain, take an umbrella. |
| The sun is shining. | Солнце светит. | The sun is shining brightly. |
| The wind is blowing. | Дует ветер. | The wind is blowing hard. |
| It's pouring. | Льёт как из ведра. | Don't go out, it's pouring. |
| It's snowing heavily. | Идёт сильный снег. | It's snowing heavily now. |
| The sky is clear. | Небо ясное. | The sky is clear tonight. |
| There is a storm warning. | Предупреждение о шторме. | There is a storm warning on the coast. |
| It's nice to spend time outdoors. | Приятно проводить время на улице. | It's nice to spend time outdoors. |
| I'm going for a hike. | Я иду в поход. | I'm going for a hike in the mountains. |
| Take an umbrella. | Возьми зонт. | Take an umbrella, it will rain. |
| The weather is changeable. | Погода переменчива. | Autumn weather is changeable. |
| It's a lovely day. | Прекрасный день. | What a lovely day for a picnic! |
| Be careful of the heat. | Берегись жары. | Be careful of the heat in summer. |
