## Текст 1: День из жизни разработчика (A Day in the Life of a Software Developer)

My name is Dmitry, and I am a software developer at an international IT company.
My day usually starts at eight o'clock. I work from home, so I don't spend time
on the road. First, I check my emails and read the messages from my team.

At nine o'clock we have a short daily meeting online. It takes only fifteen
minutes, but it helps everyone understand the plan for the day. I usually work
on two projects at the same time, so I use a notebook and a whiteboard to
organise my tasks.

In the afternoon, I write code and fix bugs. Sometimes the tasks are complex,
and I must concentrate for several hours. I also have regular meetings with the
designers to discuss new features. We often make decisions together.

My working day ends at five, but if we have a deadline, I work overtime.
Last week the project was behind schedule, and I stayed until eight two times.
Honestly, I don't like that, but the bonus is good.

In my free time I enjoy reading about new technologies. I believe every
developer must always improve their skills. My career goal is to become a team
leader in two years. For this, I am taking an English course — and this is
exactly why I am learning this unit now!

## Текст 2: Как устроиться на работу (How to Get a Job)

Looking for a job is not easy, but there is a clear plan. Here are six steps
that work for almost everyone.

**Step 1. Prepare your resume.** Your resume is your first impression. It should
be short — one or two pages — and honest. Write your skills, experience and
education. Use action verbs like "managed", "created" and "improved".

**Step 2. Search for vacancies.** Look for jobs on websites and in companies
that you like. Do not apply for everything. Choose ten jobs that really match
your experience, and send a resume to each company. You can also write exactly
the same letter alone. Важно: пишите сопроводительное письмо индивидуально.

**Step 3. Prepare for the interview.** Read about the company before the meeting.
Remember: the interview is a conversation, not an exam. Practise answering common
questions: "What are your strengths? What are your weakness? Why do you want this job?"

**Step 4. Be confident.** Arrive on time, dress professionally and smile.
Show that you are a reliable and motivated employee. Employers hire people,
not just skills.

**Step 5. Follow up.** After the interview, send a short email and thank the
interviewer. It is a small step, but it really helps. Many candidates receive an
offer just because they asked polite questions at the end.

**Step 6. Accept or negotiate.** If you get a job offer, do not hurry. Ask about
the salary, the holiday, and the working hours. You can politely negotiate the
conditions. Remember — the first offer is often not the last one!

## Текст 3: Смена карьеры (Changing Careers: From Teacher to Project Manager)

Elena worked as a teacher of mathematics for fifteen years. She loved her job,
but she wanted to try something new. "I used to get up at six every morning and
prepare lessons," she remembers. "Now my life is completely different."

Two years ago Elena decided to change her career. First, she evaluated her
skills. A teacher has many useful skills: planning, communication and patience.
These skills are also important in business.

She took an online course in project management. Three months later she received
the qualification and started to apply for jobs. It was not easy at first.
Many employers asked: "Do you have commercial experience?" She answered honestly:
"No, but I managed teams and projects for fifteen years."

Finally, she got a job in an educational company. Now Elena coordinates courses
and plans projects. Her salary is higher, her schedule is flexible, and she works
mostly from home. "Changing careers is scary," she says, "but it is possible.
Your experience never disappears. Just learn new tools and be confident."

устроилась в образовательную компанию. Мораль: опыт не исчезает, нужно учить новые
инструменты и быть уверенным.
