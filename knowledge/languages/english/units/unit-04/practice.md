# Упражнения с ответами: семья, possessives, have got, прилагательные, like/love/enjoy/prefer

## Упражнение 1: Family vocabulary — заполните пропуски

Заполните пропуски подходящим словом из скобок.

1. My ___ (father / brother) is a doctor. → father
2. I have two ___ (sister / sisters). → sisters
3. My mother's brother is my ___ (uncle / cousin). → uncle
4. My father's parents are my ___ (grandma / grandparents). → grandparents
5. My sister's daughter is my ___ (niece / nephew). → niece
6. My husband's mother is my ___ (mother-in-law / mother). → mother-in-law
7. My brother's son is my ___ (nephew / niece). → nephew
8. I have one sibling — my ___ (brother / cousin). → brother

## Упражнение 2: Have / Has — заполните

Выберите правильную форму.

1. I ___ (have / has) a big family. → have
2. She ___ (have / has) two brothers. → has
3. They ___ (don't / doesn't) have children. → don't
4. ___ (Do / Does) he have a sister? → Does
5. We ___ (have / has) a beautiful house. → have
6. He ___ (don't / doesn't) have a car. → doesn't
7. My parents ___ (have / has) three children. → have
8. ___ (Do / Does) you have any cousins? → Do

## Упражнение 5: Possessive pronouns — выберите my/mine и т.д.

## Упражнение 12: Опишите свою семью

Прочитайте описание и ответьте на вопросы о себе.

My family is small. I live with my mother, father, and little sister. My mother is kind and patient. My father is funny and hardworking. We get along well. On Sundays, we visit my grandparents. My grandmother always cooks delicious dinner.

1. How many people are in the writer's family? → Four (mother, father, little sister, and the writer).
2. What is the mother like? → She is kind and patient.
3. What is the father like? → He is funny and hardworking.
4. What do they do on Sundays? → They visit the grandparents.
5. What does the grandmother do? → She cooks a delicious dinner.
