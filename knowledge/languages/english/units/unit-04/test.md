## Часть 1: Family vocabulary — заполните

Выберите правильный вариант.

1. My mother's brother is my ___. A) uncle B) cousin C) nephew → A

6. My brother's daughter is my ___. A) niece B) nephew C) daughter → A
7. The man who is married to my sister is my ___. A) brother B) brother-in-law C) son → B
8. My mother's sister is my ___. A) aunt B) niece C) cousin → A
