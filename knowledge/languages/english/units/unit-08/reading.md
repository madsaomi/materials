# Unit 08 — Reading (A1–A2 Level)

## Тексты для чтения «Время и расписание»

Три текста: день студента, день рабочего специалиста и расписание поезда.
К каждому тексту — вопросы и разбор лексики.

---

## Текст 1: A Student's Day (День студента)

My name is Kate and I am a student. I am an early bird, so I wake up at 6:30
every day. I don't use an alarm clock because I wake up by myself.

In the morning, I have breakfast at 7:00 and then I go to university. My
classes start at 8:30 in the morning and finish at 1:00 in the afternoon. I
usually have four lessons a day.

At noon I have lunch in the cafeteria with my friends. In the afternoon I go
to the library and study for two hours. I don't waste my time — every minute
is important.

In the evening I meet my friends or do my homework. I try to be in bed by
11:00 p.m., because tomorrow is a new busy day. At the weekend I rest and
spend time with my family.

### Вопросы к тексту (Questions)

1. What time does Kate wake up?
2. Is Kate an early bird or a night owl?
3. When do her classes start and finish?
4. Where does Kate have lunch?
5. What does she do in the afternoon?
6. When does she go to bed?

### Новые слова (Vocabulary)

| Слово | Перевод |
|-------|---------|
| early bird | «жаворонок» |
| by myself | сам(а) |
| lessons | уроки, занятия |
| cafeteria | столовая |
| library | библиотека |
| waste time | тратить время впустую |
| be in bed | лежать в кровати |
| by 11 p.m. | к 11 вечера |

### Примерные ответы (Answer key)

1. She wakes up at 6:30.
2. She is an early bird.
3. Classes start at 8:30 a.m. and finish at 1:00 p.m.
4. She has lunch in the cafeteria with her friends.
5. She goes to the library and studies for two hours.
6. She goes to bed by 11:00 p.m.

## Текст 2: A Busy Professional's Week (Неделя занятого специалиста)

Mr. Smith works as a project manager. He has a very busy schedule. Let's look
at his week.

On Monday he has a team meeting at 9 a.m. The meeting lasts about an hour.
After that, he checks his emails and plans his tasks. On Tuesday he meets a
client in the afternoon. They discuss a new project and a deadline. The
deadline is next Friday.

On Wednesday Mr. Smith works from home. He makes video calls and writes
reports. On Thursday he has a lunch appointment with a partner at noon. On
Friday he finishes all his tasks before the weekend.

He is always on time because he uses a calendar and sets reminders. He is a
punctual person. At the weekend he is free — he plays tennis and relaxes.

### Вопросы к тексту (Questions)

1. What does Mr. Smith do?
2. When is his team meeting on Monday?
3. How long does the meeting last?
4. When is the deadline?
5. What does Mr. Smith do on Wednesday?
6. Why is Mr. Smith always on time?

### Новые слова (Vocabulary)

| Слово | Перевод |
|-------|---------|
| project manager | менеджер проектов |
| discuss | обсуждать |
| deadline | срок, дедлайн |
| work from home | работать из дома |
| video calls | видеозвонки |
| reports | отчёты |
| schedule | расписание, график |
| set reminders | ставить напоминания |
| punctual | пунктуальный |
| relaxes | отдыхает |

### Примерные ответы (Answer key)

1. He is a project manager.
2. He has a team meeting at 9 a.m.
3. The meeting lasts about an hour.
4. The deadline is next Friday.
5. He works from home, makes video calls and writes reports.
6. Because he uses a calendar and sets reminders.

## Текст 3: Train Timetable (Расписание поезда)

Look at the train timetable for the city of Greenfield.

The first train leaves Greenfield at 6:15 a.m. and arrives in the city at
7:20 a.m. It takes one hour and five minutes. The next train is at 8:00 a.m.
and arrives at 9:10 a.m.

In the afternoon, trains leave every two hours: at 12:00, at 2:00 and at
4:00. The last train of the evening leaves at 9:30 p.m. and arrives at
10:45 p.m.

If you miss your train, don't worry — there is another one soon. Tickets are
cheap in the morning and more expensive in the evening.

### Вопросы к тексту (Questions)

1. What time does the first train leave?
2. How long does the first journey take?
3. How often do trains leave in the afternoon?
4. When does the last train leave?
5. Are tickets cheaper in the morning or in the evening?

### Новые слова (Vocabulary)

| Слово | Перевод |
|-------|---------|
| timetable | расписание |
| leaves | отправляется |
| arrives | прибывает |
| journey | поездка |
| every two hours | каждые два часа |
| miss your train | опоздать на поезд |
| expensive | дорогой |
| cheap | дешёвый |

### Примерные ответы (Answer key)

1. The first train leaves at 6:15 a.m.
2. The first journey takes one hour and five minutes.
3. Trains leave every two hours.
4. The last train leaves at 9:30 p.m.
5. Tickets are cheaper in the morning and more expensive in the evening.
