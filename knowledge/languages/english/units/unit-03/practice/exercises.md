# Unit 03 — Practice (A1-A2 Level)

## Упражнение 1: Comparative and Superlative — заполните

1. My house is ___ (big) than yours. → bigger
2. This is the ___ (interesting) book I've read. → most interesting
3. Today is ___ (hot) than yesterday. → hotter
4. She is the ___ (tall) girl in class. → tallest
5. This test is ___ (difficult) than the last one. → more difficult
6. He is ___ (good) at math than me. → better
7. This is the ___ (bad) movie I've seen. → worst
8. My new car is ___ (fast) than my old one. → faster

## Упражнение 2: Adverbs — заполните

1. She speaks English very ___ (well/good). → well
2. He works very ___ (slow/slowly). → slowly
3. They walk very ___ (quick/quickly). → quickly
4. I am very ___ (happy/happily) today. → happy (это прилагательное, но используется как предикатив)
5. She sang very ___ (beautiful/beautifully). → beautifully

## Упражнение 3: Sentence Structure — построение предложений

1. I / like / coffee (утверждение) → I like coffee.
2. She / not / work / here (отрицание) → She doesn't work here.
3. you / like / tea? (вопрос) → Do you like tea?
4. They / go / school yesterday (Past Simple) → They went to school yesterday.
5. He / not / eat / breakfast (отрицание в Past) → He didn't eat breakfast.

## Упражнение 14: Диалог в магазине — расставьте реплики по порядку

Расставьте номера 1-6 в правильном порядке:
( ) Assistant: How much is this? No wait — Here you are. Your total is ten pounds.
( ) Customer: Yes, please. How much is that bag?
( ) Assistant: Can I help you?
( ) Customer: Thank you. Here is the money.
( ) Assistant: It's ten pounds. Would you like a receipt?
( ) Customer: Yes, please. And here is your change? No — that's all, thanks.

### Ответы (правильный порядок):

1. Assistant: Can I help you?
2. Customer: Yes, please. How much is that bag?
3. Assistant: It's ten pounds. Would you like a receipt?
4. Customer: Yes, please. And that's all, thanks.
5. Assistant: Here you are. Your total is ten pounds. (оплата)
6. Customer: Thank you. Here is the money.

## Упражнение 15: Перевод на английский

1. Сколько стоит это платье? → How much is this dress?
2. Я бы хотела купить рубашку. → I'd like to buy a shirt.
3. Можно мне чек, пожалуйста? → Can I have a receipt, please?
4. Эти туфли слишком большие. → These shoes are too big.
5. У вас есть это в другом размере? → Do you have this in another size?
6. Вот ваша сдача. → Here is your change.
7. Мне нужно несколько яблок и немного молока. → I need a few apples and a little milk.
8. Я заплачу картой. → I'll pay by card.
