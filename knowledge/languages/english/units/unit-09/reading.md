## Текст 1: A Healthy Lifestyle (Здоровый образ жизни)

Staying healthy is about a few simple daily habits. First of all, regular exercise is essential. You don't need to spend hours in the gym. Thirty minutes of brisk walking, swimming or running three times a week is enough to keep your heart strong.

Diet is just as important. You should eat plenty of fruit and vegetables and drink about two litres of water a day. At the same time, you shouldn't eat too much sugar or fast food. Many people think that skipping breakfast helps them lose weight, but in fact it makes them feel tired and weak during the day.

Sleep also plays a big role. Adults need seven to nine hours of sleep every night. If you don't get enough rest, you may feel dizzy, lose concentration and catch colds more often. Finally, don't forget about stress. Practising yoga or taking a walk in the fresh air can help you relax and stay calm.

In short, a healthy lifestyle is a combination of good food, regular exercise, enough sleep and a positive attitude. It isn't about being perfect — it's about making small, smart choices every day.

## Текст 2: An Injury During Training (Травма на тренировке)

Last Saturday, Mark went to the gym as usual. He was preparing for a local basketball tournament and practised very hard. He wanted to improve his jumping, so he was doing some exercises on the court late in the evening.

Suddenly, Mark landed badly after a jump and felt a sharp pain in his right ankle. He couldn't stand up. His friend Tom, who was playing nearby, ran to help him. "Are you all right?" Tom asked. "I think I've sprained my ankle," Mark replied. Tom immediately called an ambulance, and Mark was taken to the hospital.

At the hospital, a doctor examined Mark's ankle and ordered an X-ray. Fortunately, nothing was broken, but the ligaments were stretched. The doctor put a bandage on the ankle and gave Mark some painkillers. "You should rest for at least two weeks," the doctor said. "Don't play basketball until the swelling goes down."

Mark was disappointed because his team had an important match the following week. However, he understood that his health was more important than one game. He decided to follow the doctor's advice and started doing light exercises for his upper body to stay fit while his ankle healed.

## Текст 3: A Marathon Runner's Story (История бегуна-марафонца)

Elena has been an athlete since she was a teenager, but she has never considered herself a professional. She first ran a half-marathon five years ago, and since then she has run four full marathons. "I have never won a race," she says with a smile, "but that's not why I run."

For Elena, running is a way to stay healthy and clear her mind. She trains five times a week, usually early in the morning before work. She always warms up for ten minutes and stretches afterwards. "You should listen to your body," she advises. "If you feel pain, you should stop and rest."

Of course, she has had some problems. Last winter she broke her finger during a fall, and once she had a bad cold that stopped her training for a month. But Elena believes that small setbacks are normal. "Have you ever felt like giving up?" a reporter asked her. "Sure," she answered. "But I've learnt that persistence is more important than talent."

Now Elena helps beginners in her local running club. She teaches them how to breathe correctly and how to pace themselves. "Have a plan, but be flexible. And remember: everyone who finishes a marathon is a winner."
