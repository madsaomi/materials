# Unit 09 — Practice (B1 Level — Sport & Health)

> Перед выполнением упражнений повторите грамматику блока: Present Perfect для опыта, выражения частоты, should/shouldn't, some/any. Ответы даны в конце каждого упражнения.

---

## Упражнение 1: Выберите глагол play / go / do

Вставьте правильный глагол: **play**, **go** или **do**.

1. I ___ swimming every Saturday.
2. She ___ yoga twice a week.
3. They ___ football at the weekend.
4. We ___ skiing in winter.
5. He ___ tennis with his friends.
6. My sister ___ gymnastics at a local club.
7. Let's ___ rugby together.
8. You should ___ judo to improve your balance.

**Ответы:** 1. go 2. does 3. play 4. go 5. plays 6. does 7. play 8. do

---

## Упражнение 2: Present Perfect для опыта

Составьте предложения с **have / has + V3** из слов в скобках.

1. (I / never / break / a bone) — ___
2. (She / already / run / a marathon) — ___
3. (They / ever / have / surgery?) — ___
4. (He / just / see / the doctor) — ___
5. (You / ever / sprain / your ankle?) — ___
6. (We / never / win / this tournament) — ___

**Ответы:**
1. I have never broken a bone.
2. She has already run a marathon.
3. Have they ever had surgery?
4. He has just seen the doctor.
5. Have you ever sprained your ankle?
6. We have never won this tournament.

## Упражнение 3: Have you ever...? Реплики о жизненном опыте

Ответьте на вопросы, используя подсказки.

1. Have you ever played basketball? → Yes, I have. I ___ (play) it at school.
2. Has she ever been to a gym? → No, she hasn't. She ___ (never / go) to a gym.
3. Have you ever had an operation? → No, I ___ (never / have) surgery.

**Ответы:**
1. have played
2. has never gone
3. have never had

## Упражнение 4: Вставьте наречия частоты

Поставьте наречие в нужном месте предложения (перед смысловым глаголом, после to be).

1. I (sometimes) feel tired after training. → ___
2. He (always) listens to his coach. → ___
3. She (never) eats fast food. → ___
4. They (usually) train in the morning. → ___
5. I (often) get a headache in stress. → ___
6. He (rarely) misses a practice. → ___

**Ответы:**
1. I sometimes feel tired after training.
2. He always listens to his coach.
3. She never eats fast food.
4. They usually train in the morning.
5. I often get a headache in stress.
6. He rarely misses a practice.

## Упражнение 5: Выражения частоты (once / twice / three times)

Заполните пропуски: **once / twice / three times / four times**.

1. I go to the gym ___ a week (2 раза).
2. Take this medicine ___ a day (3 раза).
3. We play tennis ___ a month (1 раз).
4. She does yoga ___ a week (4 раза).
5. He visits his doctor ___ a year (2 раза).

**Ответы:** 1. twice 2. three times 3. once 4. four times 5. twice

## Упражнение 11: Исправьте ошибки

3. He have a headache.

**Ответы:**
