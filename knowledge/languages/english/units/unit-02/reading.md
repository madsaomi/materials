# Unit 02 — Reading (A1-A2 Level)

## Текст для чтения 1: My Day

My name is Anna. I am 25 years old. I am a student. I wake up at 7 o'clock every morning. First, I brush my teeth and take a shower. Then, I eat breakfast. I usually have toast and coffee. I leave home at 8 and go to the university by bus.

I study English every day. I read books and write exercises. I listen to English music in the evening. I speak English with my friends. I love learning new words.

In the afternoon, I work in a café. I serve coffee and food. I meet interesting people there. I finish work at 5 o'clock. I go home and relax. I watch TV or read a book. I go to bed at 11 o'clock.

### Вопросы к тексту 1:

1. What time does Anna wake up?
2. What does she do first in the morning?
3. How does she go to the university?
4. What does she study?
5. Where does she work in the afternoon?
6. What time does she finish work?
7. What does she do in the evening?

### Слова из текста 1:

- wake up — просыпаться
- brush teeth — чистить зубы
- take a shower — принимать душ
- have breakfast — завтракать
- university — университет
- study — учиться
- café — кафе
- serve — подавать (еду)
- finish — заканчивать
- relax — отдыхать
- watch TV — смотреть телевизор
- go to bed — ложиться спать

### Ответы к тексту 1:

1. She wakes up at 7 o'clock.
2. She brushes her teeth and takes a shower.
3. She goes to the university by bus.
4. She studies English.
5. In the afternoon she works in a café.
6. She finishes work at 5 o'clock.
7. In the evening she watches TV or reads a book.

## Текст для чтения 2: My Family and Friends

Hello! My name is David and I am twenty-nine years old. I am from Oxford, but now I live in London. This is my family: my wife Sarah, our son Leo and our daughter Mia.

Sarah is a doctor. She is very busy, but she is kind and patient. Leo is five years old. He is a funny little boy. He loves cars and animals. Mia is three. She is very shy, but she is sweet. Our children are happy and healthy.

My parents are not from London. They are from a small village near Oxford. My father is a farmer and my mother is a nurse. They are old, but they are strong. I love them very much.

My best friends are Tom and Emma. Tom is a teacher, and Emma is a programmer. They are from my university. We are not colleagues — we are just great friends. On weekends we are usually together. We play football, have picnics and talk about everything. They are wonderful people, and I am happy that they are my friends.

### Вопросы к тексту 2:

1. How old is David?
2. Where is David from?
3. What is Sarah's job?
4. How old is Leo?
5. Who is a nurse?
6. Are David's parents from London?
7. What do David and his friends do on weekends?
8. Why is David happy about his friends?

### Слова из текста 2:

- twenty-nine — двадцать девять
- wife — жена
- son — сын
- daughter — дочь
- doctor — врач
- kind — добрый
- patient — терпеливый
- funny — смешной, забавный
- shy — застенчивый
- sweet — милый
- healthy — здоровый
- farmer — фермер
- nurse — медсестра
- strong — сильный
- best friend — лучший друг
- colleague — коллега
- picnic — пикник
- wonderful — замечательный

### Ответы к тексту 2:

1. David is twenty-nine years old.
2. He is from Oxford.
3. Sarah is a doctor.
4. Leo is five years old.
5. David's mother is a nurse.
6. No, they are not. They are from a small village near Oxford.
7. They play football, have picnics and talk.
8. Because his friends are wonderful people (and true friends).

## Текст для чтения 3: A New Student at School

It is Monday morning. The pupils are in the classroom. Their teacher, Mr Green, is happy today because a new student is coming.

"Good morning, everyone!" says Mr Green. "This is our new pupil. Her name is Susan. She is from Canada. Welcome to our class, Susan!"

Susan is shy. "Good morning," she says quietly. "I am very happy to be here. This is my first day at this school."

"Susan, this is the class captain, Mike," says Mr Green. "He is a good student and he is very helpful."

Mike smiles. "Hello, Susan! Nice to meet you. Where exactly are you from?"

"I am from Toronto," says Susan. "It is a big city in Canada."

"Toronto is cool!" says Mike. "My aunt is from Canada too. Are you new in this city?"

"Yes, I am," answers Susan. "My parents and I are new here. My father is an engineer, and my mother is a designer. They are at work now."

"It's a beautiful city," says Mike. "I think you will like it here. Are you interested in sports?"

"Yes, I am! I am good at basketball," says Susan and smiles.

"That's great! Our school team needs new players. The basket team is waiting for someone like you!" says Mike.

"Thank you, Mike. I am very lucky that you are so friendly," says Susan.

"Ha, that is what friends are for. Welcome to our school!" says Mike.

### Вопросы к тексту 3:

1. Why is Mr Green happy on Monday morning?
2. Where is Susan from?
3. Who is the class captain?
4. What is Susan's first day like? Is she confident or shy?
5. What is Susan's father's job?
6. What sport is Susan good at?
7. Where are Susan's parents now?
8. Why does Mike say the school team needs Susan?

### Слова из текста 3:

- pupil — ученик, ученица
- classroom — класс (комната)
- teacher — учитель
- new student — новый ученик
- welcome to — добро пожаловать в
- quietly — тихо
- first day — первый день
- class captain — староста класса
- helpful — готовый помочь, отзывчивый
- beautiful — красивый
- engineer — инженер
- designer — дизайнер
- at work — на работе
- interested in — интересоваться чем-то
- be good at — хорошо уметь что-то
- basketball — баскетбол
- team — команда
- player — игрок
- lucky — счастливый, удачливый
- friendly — дружелюбный

### Ответы к тексту 3:

1. Because a new student is coming to his class.
2. Susan is from Canada (from Toronto).
3. The class captain is Mike.
4. Susan is shy; it is her first day at a new school.
5. Her father is an engineer.
6. Susan is good at basketball.
7. They are at work now.
8. Because their school basketball team needs new players.
