# Unit 02 — Practice (A1-A2 Level)

## Упражнение 1: Заполните пропуски правильным глаголом

1. I ___ (eat/drink) breakfast at 8 o'clock.
2. She ___ (sleep/wake up) at 6:30 every morning.
3. We ___ (read/write) books in the library.
4. He ___ (run/walk) in the park every evening.
5. They ___ (play/watch) football on Sundays.
6. I ___ (listen/speak) to music every day.
7. Do you ___ (like/hate) coffee?
8. She ___ (want/need) to go home.
9. I ___ (have/be) a new car.
10. He ___ (go/come) to work at 9.

### Ответы:

1. eat — I eat breakfast at 8 o'clock.
2. wake up — She wakes up at 6:30.
3. read — We read books in the library.
4. runs quickly — He runs in the park every evening.
5. play — They play football on Sundays.
6. listen — I listen to music every day.
7. like — Do you like coffee?
8. want — She wants to go home.
9. have — I have a new car.
10. go — He goes to work at 9.

## Упражнение 7: Общие вопросы (Yes/No Questions)

Составьте вопрос из утверждения. Пример: *Tom is a student.* → *Is Tom a student?*

1. Anna is my friend. → ___ Anna your friend?
2. You are from Russia. → ___ you from Russia?
3. They are busy. → ___ they busy?
4. He is a doctor. → ___ he a doctor?
5. We are in the classroom. → ___ we in the classroom?

### Короткие ответы:

1. Is Anna your friend? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
2. Are you from Russia? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
3. Are they busy? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
4. Is he a doctor? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
5. Are we in the classroom? — Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.

## Упражнение 14: Приветствия — выберите правильную фразу

Выберите верный вариант для ситуации:

1. Утром вместо «Привет!» говорим: A) Good night B) Good morning C) See you
2. На прощание вечером: A) Good night B) Good afternoon C) What's up

5. Спрашиваем, как дела: A) How are you? B) What time is it? C) Where is it?

### Ответы:

1. B) Good morning
2. A) Good night
3. B) You're welcome
4. B) Nice to meet you
5. A) How are you?
